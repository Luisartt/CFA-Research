RNS Number : 3416P
Grupo Bimbo, S.A.B. DE C.V.
09 October 2019
 

 

 Grupo Bimbo, S.A.B. de C.V. (the "Issuer") 

 

 

 

                USD 800000000 4.875% Notes due 2020 - GEM (the "Notes")
