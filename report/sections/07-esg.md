# ESG

[[figs: esg_sp, esg_lseg, emissions, governance]]

Bimbo's 2025 double-materiality analysis found 11 material topics (Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, pp. 146-147). We propose five and rank them by their link to value: climate and input costs, nutrition and health regulation, water, packaging and circular economy, and sustainable sourcing (team estimate). Climate through wheat and nutrition regulation in Mexico move value; governance moves the multiple. The two main ESG ratings disagree: the S&P Global ESG Score is 59 in 2026 against a food-industry average of 29 ({fig:esg_sp}), while LSEG rates Bimbo A (84.62), 12th of 416 food and tobacco companies ({fig:esg_lseg}) (S&P Capital IQ, 2026; LSEG, 2026). LSEG scores the presence of policies and S&P the quality of disclosure, so LSEG alone may overstate governance (team estimate).

## Environmental | Climate and input costs matter most

Scope 1 emissions were 1,240,655 t in FY2025 (+8.6%) and Scope 3 emissions 11,764,297 t (+3.0%) (LSEG, 2026, preliminary data). The 2025 Scope 3 target (-12.5% against 2019) was missed. On the restated baseline, Scope 1 is 1.4% above 2019, and the 2019 Scope 3 baseline itself was restated 42% higher; the 2030 targets are -50% for Scope 1 and -28% for Scope 3 ({fig:emissions}) (LSEG, 2026; Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, p. 169). S&P's climate-strategy score fell from 81 to 54 in 2026 (S&P Capital IQ, 2026). The integrated report states that 100% of electricity is renewable, partly through certificates; the LSEG annex says 99%, a difference to clear before quoting. Limited assurance by KPMG does not cover emissions (Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, pp. 176-180).

Water and packaging carry regulatory risk. By two counts, 89 to 101 plants sit in areas of high water stress; the December 2025 water-law reform limits transfers of concessions; and water use per tonne was 7% below 2019 at end-2024 against a 20% target for 2030 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 39, 93; 2026e, pp. 12, 98). The January 2026 Circular Economy Law creates producer responsibility for packaging, 99% of which is recyclable, but its cost is not yet set (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 38-39, 91-92).

## Social | Health and labour

Bimbo has 147,873 employees on payroll in 39 countries, or 170,642 including contractors (Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, p. 63). Its recordable injury rate fell from 2.38 in 2021 to 1.59 in 2025 (internal audit only), and women hold 30.5% of leadership roles (Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, pp. 64, 75-76). Some 48% of sales have an optimal nutrition profile, and Bimbo ranks fourth globally in the 2024 Access to Nutrition Initiative index (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 29-31, 79, 89; 2026e, p. 11). The social issue that reaches value is Mexico's labour reform, with a 46-hour week in 2027 and 40 hours by 2030 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 48).

## Governance | A controlled company

Six holding vehicles hold 72.03% of the shares. The chair is executive, a family member and has a casting vote; 7 of 18 directors are independent (38.9% against a 25% legal floor) and seven carry the Servitje surname (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 171, 181-182; LSEG, 2026). The audit committee is fully independent (5 of 5). Related-party transactions were MXN 9,823 million, 2.3% of sales (Grupo Bimbo, 2026f, note 15). The by-laws require board approval for any transfer that takes a holder to 3% and set a 30% tender-offer trigger with board approval, and a 10% stake is needed to call a meeting, which is 36% of the free float (Grupo Bimbo, 2026g, arts. 17, 17 bis, 47-48; team model). Two CEO changes in 18 months (May 2024 and November 2025) add key-person risk. Our governance scorecard gives 31 of 64 points, 48% ({fig:governance}) (team estimate).

## Link to Valuation

- **Climate and input costs** act through gross margin: losing 50 bp costs MXN 3.72 per share if sustained from FY2026E and MXN 3.55 from FY2027E (team model).
- **Nutrition regulation** acts through the Mexican margin: -100 to -150 bp costs MXN 2.79 to 4.19 (team model).
- **Governance** acts through the multiple, not the discount rate: each 0.5x is worth MXN 5.90, and we add no WACC premium because the market already pays a premium to the bakers (team model).
- **Carbon price or packaging fees** of MXN 1 billion a year from FY2027 would cost MXN 1.43; a one-off claim of MXN 1 billion, MXN 0.24; and the Scope 3 step-up on the 23L bond is worth less than MXN 0.01 per share (team model).
- Water, packaging and sourcing are material but not quantifiable, because Bimbo does not publish its sustainability capex (Grupo Bimbo, 2026e, p. 168).

## Opportunities | From compliance to leadership

The double-materiality analysis gives a framework to prioritise, and 85% of palm volume is now deforestation-free, up from 70% in December 2024 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 40, 95-96; 2026e, pp. 146-147). The split of CEO and chair since May 2024 and a fully independent audit committee are positives. If Bimbo turns its disclosures into assured emission cuts (emissions are not assured and SBTi validation is paused), part of the ESG discount could narrow (team estimate).
