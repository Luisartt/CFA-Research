# Executive Summary

[[box: keydata]]
[[figs: football]]

**HOLD (provisional); the team will set the target price.** At MXN 58.60 (16 October 2026) Grupo Bimbo trades at 6.9x last-twelve-month EV/EBITDA, a premium to the core bakers (5.5x) and a discount to all eligible peers (8.3x) (S&P Capital IQ, 2026; team model). Our DCF values the shares at MXN 46.42 today and MXN 51.42 in twelve months, an expected return of -10.5% including the MXN 1.05 dividend (team model). That is the edge of our SELL band (-10% or lower). The price already requires a 6.95x exit multiple on FY2030E EBITDA, which is where the stock trades today (team model). The rating therefore rests on one judgement: whether Bimbo keeps a premium to the core bakers. A clean HOLD (expected return between -5% and +10%) needs an exit multiple between 6.16x and 6.82x. We have not set that multiple; it is the team's decision ({fig:football}).

Three theses, validated by the team, frame the debate (see Investment Summary). First, North America's margin problem is being corrected: closing the gap to Flowers Foods is worth +MXN 4.22 per share. Second, 2027 margins are at risk from wheat: losing 50 bp for good costs MXN 3.55. Third, organic growth falls short of the model: a 1-point miss in North America and 2 points in Mexico costs MXN 3.29. All three together move the value by -MXN 2.65 (team model).

The catalysts are 3Q26 results in late October, FY2026 results and 2027 guidance in February 2027, wheat entering the 2027 hedge book and the shareholders' meeting by end-April 2027 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 62). The main risk is the multiple: each 0.5x on the exit multiple is worth MXN 5.90 per share (team model).
