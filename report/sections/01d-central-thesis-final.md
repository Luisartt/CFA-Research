# Central thesis — final (Luisart's text, figures from TP12m_v2)

Text: Luisart's. Figures: `analisis-dcf-2026-10-26/precio-objetivo/BIMBOA_statements_2021-2030_v2_pivot_waccV2_TP12m_v2.xlsx`. Changed figures are marked in the table below; the wording is unchanged.

---

**Bimbo is worth what it costs today — due to growth of about 3% once acquisitions and currency are stripped out and a new management that must still prove the margin it inherited, offset by costs that are covered into 2027 and absorbable after it.**

The recovery is real. In 3Q26 the group margin reached 15.7%, 100 bp above a year earlier, leverage fell to 2.4x and management raised its 2026 margin guide for the third time (Grupo Bimbo, 3Q26 earnings call; 2026h). But the base is softer than the headline. Net sales excluding currency grew 4.4% in 9M26, of which about 1.1 points were acquisitions; and the peso, which cut reported sales by 2.2% in those nine months, turns in our model into 1.46 of the 4.75 points of FY2027E growth. Of the 120 bp of margin we model to FY2027E, 114 come from two lines that fall almost to zero, under a CEO who took over in November 2025 a recovery that was already under way (team model).

Costs are what keep this from being a sell. Raw materials are fully hedged for 4Q26 and almost fully for 1H27, and even wheat 35% higher for good would cost about MXN 14.75 of the MXN 66.13 target, 22% of it (Grupo Bimbo, 3Q26 earnings call; team model). That cushion explains why 2026 looks easy, and it sets the date of the test. From 4Q26 Wickbold stops counting as acquired growth, in the second half of 2027 the hedges run out, and 2027 is the first year that the new management cannot lean on what it inherited. The market already pays for the outcome: the share rose 10.7% between 16 and 27 October to MXN 64.85, and at that price our 12-month target of MXN 66.13 plus the MXN 1.12 dividend leaves an expected return of 3.7%, well below our 12.26% cost of equity. We rate Bimbo HOLD, not because the recovery is in doubt, but because the price already pays for it and only 2027 can show whether it deserves more.
