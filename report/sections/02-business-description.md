# Business Overview

[[figs: sales_region, sales_vs_ebitda]]

Grupo Bimbo, S.A.B. de C.V. is the world's largest baking company by sales, with 3.7% of the global bakery market, 1.2x the next competitor (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 107-109). It runs 249 plants and more than 54,000 delivery routes in 39 countries and sells more than 9,000 products under over 100 brands (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 14, 100). FY2025 net sales were MXN 426,952 million (+4.6%) and Adjusted EBITDA was MXN 59,456 million, a 13.9% margin (Grupo Bimbo, 2026f, p. 85; team model).

## Business Model

Bimbo bakes fresh bread, sweet baked goods, cookies, tortillas and salty snacks and delivers them to stores on its own routes. Fresh product lasts about 14 days, so the delivery network, not the recipe, is the barrier to entry (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 100, 110). The network is densest in Mexico, where Bimbo operates all routes directly. It leads packaged bread (18.2% share) and sweet baked goods (28.2%) and is second in salty snacks (18.4%) (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 111, 113). Customers are modern retail, traditional stores, convenience chains and quick-service restaurants. Walmart is about 15% of sales and no other customer exceeds 10% (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 103). New products supplied 12.6% of FY2025 sales (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 87).

## Key Operating Segments

Bimbo reports four regions (Grupo Bimbo, 2026f, p. 85). Shares are of FY2025 net sales and Adjusted EBITDA ({fig:sales_region}, {fig:sales_vs_ebitda}).

- **North America** (44.6% of sales, 28.8% of EBITDA, 9.0% margin): Bimbo Bakeries USA (54 bakeries, about 27% of US fresh bread, buns and rolls), Barcel USA, Bimbo QSR and Bimbo Canada (17 bakeries, 13.3% share) (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 82-84; Flowers Foods, 2026).
- **Mexico** (32.4% of sales, 55.4% of EBITDA, 20.4% margin): the Bimbo and Barcel brands (including Takis) and 38 plants (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 84-85; 2026e, p. 17).
- **Europe, Asia and Africa** (12.7%, 9.8%, 10.8%): Iberia, the United Kingdom, Romania, China, India and Africa, plus bread for quick-service restaurants in about 40 countries; 92 plants after the 2025 acquisitions (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 85-87).
- **Latin America** (10.3%, 6.0%, 8.1%): 15 countries and 42 plants; Wickbold (Brazil) joined in October 2025 and its integration costs weigh on margin (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 85; 2026d, p. 5).

Mexico earns 55% of EBITDA on 32% of sales; North America, the largest region, earns 29%. The mix is shifting: North America was 52% of sales in FY2021 and 42.6% over the last twelve months to June 2026, partly through acquisitions and peso translation (Grupo Bimbo, 2026d, p. 3; team model).

## Strategy, Capital and Ownership

Management is rebuilding North America's profitability. It closed 11 bakeries worldwide in 2024 and reports "record productivity benefits" since 3Q25 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 76-77; 2026d, p. 5). Capex fell from MXN 34,754 million in FY2023 to MXN 22,530 million in FY2025, and 2026 guidance was lowered to US$1.0-1.2 billion (Grupo Bimbo, 2026f, p. 86; 2Q26 earnings call). Leverage is 2.5x net debt/EBITDA on the company's definition and the ratings are BBB+ (S&P), BBB+ (Fitch) and Baa1 (Moody's) (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, p. 82; 2026d, p. 2).

Six holding vehicles control 72.03% of the shares, led by Normaciel with 40.96%; the free float is 27.6% (Grupo Bimbo, 2026a, pp. 181-182; LSEG, 2026). The board has 18 directors, 7 of them independent. Daniel Servitje Montull is Executive Chairman and Alejandro Rodriguez Bas has been Group CEO since November 2025 (Grupo Bimbo, 2026b, p. 2).
