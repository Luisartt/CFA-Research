## Pillar 2: Costs can rise without breaking the margin — Bimbo has already absorbed a shock almost four times larger

- **A larger shock, already absorbed.** Between FY2021 and FY2023, raw materials rose from 30.0% to 32.8% of net sales, an increase of 279 bp, and gross margin fell 206 bp, from 53.5% to 51.4%. Adjusted EBITDA margin never left a range of 13.4% to 14.0% in FY2021-FY2025 (Grupo Bimbo, 2024, note 21; 2026f, pp. 13, 82, 85; team model).

- **Our model asks for far less.** Raw materials rise from 29.9% of net sales in FY2026E to 30.6% in FY2027E, 76 bp, about a quarter of the FY2021-FY2023 shock. The model already passes through the 25.9% increase in wheat in 2026 with a one-year lag (R-squared 0.70 vs 0.48 for the same year). That increase is why gross margin falls 58 bp, from 52.7% to 52.1%, in FY2027E (Figure 17) (IMF, 2026; team model).

- **The market expects the same.** Consensus expects a FY2027 gross margin of 52.0% against our 52.1% (S&P Capital IQ, 2026; team model). A cost increase of this size is already in the price, so it is not a reason to sell.

- **Hedges give visibility, not protection.** The CFO said the main raw materials for 4Q26 are fully hedged and those for 1H27 almost 100%, and that Bimbo never hedges 15 months ahead. He called 2027 "a challenge" for cost of sales (Grupo Bimbo, 3Q26 earnings call). Bimbo's hedging model compares the current price with its five-year average (Grupo Bimbo, 2026f, p. 9). The first quarter exposed to new prices is therefore 3Q27, and the second-half hedges are bought during 1H27.

- **Room before the rating turns.** At MXN 58.60, our optimistic 12-month target of MXN 77.10 would have to fall by MXN 12.44 (16%) before the rating turns to SELL. Wheat at its September 2026 price of US$247 per tonne takes MXN 4.74 from the base target (team model). That is why this pillar argues against selling, not for buying.

## Events that could change the rating

The rating rule sets the price lines: we would move to BUY below MXN 51.84 and to SELL above MXN 69.68, and both lines move with our value (team model). Each pillar also has a public test with a date. Each signal in Table 6 is a figure Bimbo or the market publishes. Each threshold is tied to the assumption in our model that the signal would confirm or break.

**Paragraph under the table.** We chose signals that are published, dated and tied to a single assumption. Two of them arrive with the 4Q26 results at the end of February 2027: the first quarter of growth without Wickbold as acquired growth, and management's formal 2027 margin guidance. The 1Q27 report then shows whether other expenses and integration costs actually fall. The cost pillar has no BUY signal. It is the floor of our HOLD, and cheaper wheat would not prove the growth or the margin that keep us from buying.
