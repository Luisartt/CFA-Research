# Input-Cost Commodities for a Global Packaged Bakery (Grupo Bimbo): prices as of early Oct-2026, versus the 2022 peak, YoY, and the 2026-2028 curve

Research date: 2026-10-08. All figures come from web sources dated late Sep or early Oct 2026 unless noted. FX reference: USD/MXN ~17.4-18. Sader used 17.25 in May-2026; the MXN conversions below are my own calculations at the rate shown.

**Macro backdrop for every commodity below.** A Middle East conflict is disrupting flows through the Strait of Hormuz, and Saudi Arabia's East-West bypass pipeline was attacked. Brent averaged $114/bbl in Sep-2026 and touched $131 on 15-Sep ([EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)). FAO cites "disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea" combined with climate shocks (dry weather in North America, SE Asia and India, plus El Niño) ([FAO, 2-Oct-2026](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en)).

## 1. Wheat (principal input): CBOT SRW, KC HRW, curve, WASDE, Mexico

### Takeaway
Wheat is about 35% higher YoY but still about 49% below the March-2022 peak. The futures curve is in mild contango, rising about 5% from Dec-26 to Dec-27 and about 6% to Mar-28, so the market does not price relief into 2027. USDA raised the 2026/27 US season-average farm price to $6.40/bu. Mexico imports about 72% of the wheat it consumes, so its flour costs are effectively priced in USD.

### Inferences
- Dec-26 SRW at $6.865 is about **-49%** versus the 1,350 ¢ March-2022 peak (computed). KC HRW Dec-26 at $7.385 carries a premium of about $0.52 over SRW.
- On curve shape, Dec-27 SRW (723.5) is +5.4% over Dec-26 and Mar-28 is +6.3%. That is normal carry, with **no backwardation signalling relief**. Bakers rolling hedges into 2027 lock in roughly 35% higher wheat than a year earlier.
- In MXN terms, $6.865/bu is about $252/t, or about **MXN 4,400-4,550/t** at 17.4-18.0 before basis and freight (computed: 6.865 × 36.744). Domestic producer prices of MXN 5,100-5,600/t are consistent with an import-parity basis. A stronger peso (Bimbo cited peso appreciation in 2Q26) partly offsets USD wheat inflation for the Mexican P&L.

## 2. Vegetable oils: soybean oil (biofuel policy), palm oil, FAO index

### Takeaway
Vegetable oils are the most structurally inflationary component. The FAO Vegetable Oil Index is +18% YoY, and US biofuel policy (45Z extended through 2029, RVOs finalized) underpins soyoil at roughly 67-71 ¢/lb. Palm is softer, at 12-week lows near MYR 4,500-4,600/t because Malaysian stocks are at a record.

### Cited Findings
- FAO Vegetable Oil Price Index: **198.6 points in Sep-2026**, +0.9% m/m and **+18% YoY**, mainly on palm oil — [Potatopro/FAO](https://www.potatopro.com/news/2026/fao-food-price-index-edges-september-higher-sugar-cereal-and-vegetable-oil-prices); [FAO](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en)
- CBOT soybean oil closed mostly between about 67 and 71 ¢/lb in Sep-2026, with **68.28 ¢/lb on 30-Sep-2026**. The 52-week range was 48.05-79.69 ¢/lb — [Investing.com historical data](https://www.investing.com/commodities/us-soybean-oil-historical-data), via search summary; I did not fetch the table directly
- Soyoil gained 3.2% in the week to 28-Sep. The Sep WASDE has US 2026/27 soyoil ending stocks at 1,877 M lb and stocks-to-use at 5.6%, the tightest since 2023/24 — [Farmbucks](https://farmbucks.com/futures/grain-oilseeds/soybean-oil)
- Policy: the tax law extends the 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit through 2029 and removes indirect land-use-change penalties. - Palm oil (BMD Dec contract): MYR 4,533/t on 2-Oct-2026, the lowest close since 10-Jul, and a second weekly loss. It closed at MYR 4,524 (about $1,107/t) on 7-Oct and rose to MYR 4,605 (about $1,128/t) early on 8-Oct on Indian demand and firmer crude — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40442320/palm-hits-13-week-low-on-track-for-second-straight-weekly-loss); [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40443254/palm-rises-about-2-on-india-demand-hopes-stronger-crude-dalian)

- Trading Economics palm oil: 4,661 on 8-Oct-2026, **+1.46% YoY** — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/wheat) (listed in its commodity table)

### Inferences
- Soyoil at about 68 ¢/lb is well above its 52-week low (48 ¢), which points to roughly +30-40% YoY. Policy (45Z through 2029 plus RVOs) gives it a structural floor, so I see no reversion to the pre-policy 40s-50s in 2027. Palm is the cheaper substitute and is near flat YoY.
- El Niño in 2027 is an upside risk to palm, through lower Southeast Asian output.

## 3. Sugar: ICE No.11, No.5, US No.16, Mexico

### Takeaway
Sugar has turned inflationary. No.11 is at 17-month highs (about 20 ¢/lb, +24% YoY) on supply shocks in Thailand, India, the EU and Brazil, and the 2026/27 global balance has swung to deficit. The curve stays firm into 2027-28.

### Cited Findings
- ISA daily price (average of the first three ICE No.11 positions): **20.07 ¢/lb on 7-Oct-2026** ($560.70/t), 15-day average 18.54. White sugar index (first two London No.5 positions): **25.43 ¢/lb on 7-Oct-2026**. On 1-Oct the readings were 18.46 raw and 23.32 white — [International Sugar Organization daily prices](https://www.isosugar.org/prices.php)
- Trading Economics No.11: 20.18 ¢/lb on 8-Oct-2026, -3.1% on the day, **+9.7% over one month and +24.1% YoY**. A 17-month high of 19.11 was reported on 30-Sep (later exceeded). TE forecasts 20.36 at quarter-end and 21.73 in 12 months — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/sugar)
- Supply drivers: wet weather delaying Centre-South Brazil crush, a weak Indian monsoon, Thai drought (StoneX expects Thai output down at least 17%), and EU beet output down about 19% after extreme heat. StoneX projects a 900 kt global deficit in 2026/27 — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/sugar)
- ISO projects a 2026/27 global deficit of 200 kt, after a projected 1.1 Mt surplus in 2025/26 (as cited by Barchart; the date is likely Aug-2026) — [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/futures/quotes/SB*0/futures-prices)
- FAO Sugar Price Index rose **6.1% m/m in September** — [FAO](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en)
- The sugar forward curve "stays firm into 2027–28" — [CMB News](https://commodity-board.com/sugar-market-softens-as-futures-ease-but-forward-curve-stays-firm)
- Higher oil prices lift sugar because they raise ethanol parity in Brazil. In March-2026 sugar was about 15.9 ¢, its highest since Oct-2025, on rising oil — [ISO/Barchart context](https://www.barchart.com/futures/quotes/SB*0/futures-prices)

### Inferences
- No.11 at about 20 ¢/lb is roughly 30% below the Nov-2023 cycle high of about 28 ¢ (that high is from memory and not verified this session), so sugar is not at a peak. It is clearly inflationary YoY for 2027 contracting.
- With oil elevated (Brent above $100), Brazilian mills favour ethanol, which supports the sugar floor.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the US No.16 domestic sugar price or Mexico's domestic sugar price (SNIIM / CONADESUCA). I also did not find a USDA sugar outlook for FY2027.

## 5. Energy: WTI and Brent, US and Mexico diesel, Henry Hub, electricity, EIA STEO

### Takeaway
This is an acute oil and diesel shock. US retail diesel averaged $6.29/gal in September, and EIA forecasts Brent at $105 in 4Q26, easing to $84 for 2027 and $74 by 4Q27. Natural gas is the benign exception: Henry Hub is about $3.1-3.25 and forecast at $3.16 for 2027. Mexico's diesel is held near MXN 27/l by a voluntary price agreement, which shields Bimbo Mexico's fleet.

### Cited Findings
- EIA STEO Oct-2026 (inputs finalized 1-Oct-2026). Brent annual averages: 2025 $69, **2026 $96, 2027 $84**/bbl (prior STEO: $91 and $74). Brent averaged **$114 in Sep-2026** and peaked at **$131 on 15-Sep**. Forecasts are **$105 in 4Q26**, $87 by 2Q27 and $74 in 4Q27. EIA assumes 4.5 mb/d of Middle East production shut in during 4Q26 and most of it back by the end of 2Q27 — [EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)
- US retail diesel averaged **$6.29/gal in Sep-2026** and gasoline $4.35. Diesel is forecast at **$5.19 for 2026 and $4.49 for 2027**, above $6 in October and above $4 through 2027. East Coast distillate stocks were 32% below the five-year average in September — [EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)
- Retail gasoline is forecast at $3.91 for 2026 and $3.56 for 2027, versus $3.10 in 2025 — [EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)
- Henry Hub averaged $3.53 in 2025 and is forecast at **$3.48 in 2026 and $3.16 in 2027** (-9%). January 2026 averaged $7.72 after Winter Storm Fern. EIA sees $3.09 for Feb-Dec 2027, a low of $2.60 in Apr-May 2027 and $3.90 in Dec-2027 — [EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)

### Inferences
- Mexico diesel at MXN 27.01/l is about $1.50-1.55/l, or roughly **$5.70-5.90/gal** at 17.4-18.0 (computed). That is below the US September average of $6.29/gal, and it is flat versus July. The voluntary cap therefore insulates Bimbo's largest market from the diesel spike, at the cost of Pemex/fiscal (IEPS) subsidies. The policy risk is that the cap is withdrawn if oil stays above $100.
- US fleet fuel for Bimbo Bakeries USA: 2026 diesel averages $5.19 per EIA. 2027 at $4.49 is about -13%, so fuel is a **2026 headwind that turns into a 2027 tailwind** if EIA's path holds. The risk skew is to the upside given Hormuz.
- Natural gas for oven fuel is flat to down into 2027 at $3.16. Vepormas noted that Bimbo's **energy hedges** weighed on 2Q26 results ([Vepormas](https://estrategia.vepormas.com/2026/07/23/trimestral-grupo-bimbo-2t26/)), which suggests Bimbo is hedged at above-market energy prices.

## 9. Bottom line: is the basket inflationary into 2027, and what is the margin impact for Bimbo?

### Takeaway
**The basket is inflationary into 2027 versus 2026, especially in 1H27.** Wheat (+35% YoY), sugar (+24%), soyoil and vegetable oils (+18% FAO index) and packaging (oil-linked) all point up, and the curves are in contango. Partial offsets come from eggs (-23 to -29%), natural gas (-9% in 2027 per EIA), US diesel (-13% in 2027 per EIA, if Hormuz normalizes) and peso strength. On the stated assumptions below, the net raw-material inflation of roughly 5-8% in USD maps to about **150-240 bp of gross-margin headwind before pricing**, against a raw-material line equal to 30.4% of sales.

### Cited Findings (inputs to the estimate)
- Wheat YoY +34.9% (TE, 8-Oct). CBOT Dec-27 is +5.4% over Dec-26 — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/wheat); [IndexBox/USDA](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/usda-grain-futures-and-canola-bids-report-for-october-7-2026/)
- FAO cereals +17.2% YoY and vegetable oils +18% YoY — [FAO](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en)
- Sugar No.11 +24.1% YoY — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/sugar)
- Eggs: 2026 forecast -29.4% — [USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings)
- Diesel $5.19 in 2026 vs $4.49 in 2027. Henry Hub $3.48 vs $3.16 — [EIA STEO Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)
- Campbell's peer read is 5-6% input inflation for FY27 — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-campbells-q4-2026-revenue-miss-and-weak-outlook-hit-shares-93CH-4888096)

### Inferences (analyst estimates, not sourced facts)
- **Illustrative raw-material weights** (team or assumed, not disclosed by Bimbo): flour and wheat about 35-40%, fats and oils about 10-12%, sugar and sweeteners about 8-10%, packaging about 15-20%, eggs and dairy about 5%, other about 15-20%.
- **Hedge lag**: Bimbo's 2026 costs partly reflect hedges placed at the lower 2025 wheat levels. The 2026 move in spot prices (wheat +35%) therefore flows into 2027 as hedges roll, so **1H27 is likely the cost peak**.
- Under these assumptions, 2027 USD raw-material inflation is roughly **+5% to +8%**. Wheat alone at a 35-40% weight with about 15-25% realized inflation after hedging and milling adds about 5-9 pp of the basket's raw-material cost; oils and sugar add 1-2 pp; eggs subtract less than 0.5 pp; packaging is about flat to +1 pp.
- **P&L sensitivity**: each 1% of raw-material inflation is about **30 bp of gross margin** (0.304 × 1%). At +5% to +8%, that is about **150-240 bp** before pricing and productivity. Raw materials are about 64% of cost of sales, so cost of sales rises roughly 3.2-5.1% from raw materials alone. Bimbo's 2Q26 gross margin was 52.6%.
- **MXN effect**: about 72% of Mexico's wheat is imported and priced in USD. Each 1 MXN move in USD/MXN (about 5.6% at 17.8) changes Mexican wheat costs by a similar percentage. A stronger peso (around 17.4) offsets part of the USD inflation in the Mexico segment, while it reduces the MXN translation of US, North America and Europe results.
- **Distribution fuel** sits mostly in SG&A/distribution, not in raw materials. It is a 2026 headwind in the US (diesel $6.29 in September) and partly shielded in Mexico by the MXN 27/l voluntary cap. EIA's base case makes it a 2027 tailwind, with high variance tied to Hormuz.
- **Scenario framing**:
  - **Deflationary 2027**: requires Hormuz to normalize, a Black Sea truce, and a large 2027 northern-hemisphere wheat crop. The curve does not price this.
  - **Flat**: requires wheat to stay near $6.86 and oil to fall to EIA's $74 by 4Q27.
  - **Inflationary**: the base case on the current curve and peer guidance.

### Gaps
- Bimbo's actual raw-material mix, hedge coverage and tenor for 2027 are not public in the sources found.
- The BLS PPI for bakery ingredients and flour was not retrieved.
- The World Bank Oct-2026 CMO, which would give 2027-2028 food and energy forecasts, was not found. 2028 forecasts beyond the futures curve (Mar-28 wheat at 729.75 SRW and 770.50 HRW) are unavailable.
