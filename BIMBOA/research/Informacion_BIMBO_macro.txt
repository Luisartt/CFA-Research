Indicator 3 — Nominal GDP Growth % y/y: The tool does not return this as a standalone field. Where derivable from consecutive nominalGDPLCU values in the same local currency, it is computed as:

Derived formula (applied once): Nominal GDP Growth % y/y = (GDP_t / GDP_{t-1} − 1) × 100

This is marked Derived in the value_type column. For the European Union aggregate, nominalGDPLCU is returned in $B (USD billions); all other geographies use their local currency. Because the EU nominal GDP is in USD, cross-year derivations are valid within that series. Note: the first year of each series (2015) has no prior year in the dataset and is therefore N/A for the derived indicator.

Indicator 4 — consumerPriceInflation: The tool labels this field consumerPriceInflation without specifying annual average vs. end-of-period. Based on S&P Capital IQ's standard methodology for this field, it represents the annual average CPI % change. This is stated as provided by the tool; no independent verification is possible from the data payload.

Indicators not available in the tool (not attempted further):

CPI food / CPI bread and cereals
