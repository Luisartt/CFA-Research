## 2. Cross-year patterns

### 2.1 Pricing versus volume
- Management's pattern: lead with price when inflation is high, then discover elasticity 2-4 quarters later: Mexico 2013-14 (price rises, volume -6%), US 2015 (price +5% vs category, share loss), US 2018-19 (priced ahead of category, mainstream volume loss), US 2022 (three rises, first branded elasticity in 3Q22), Mexico 2022-23 (pushing the envelope, volume soft in 2H23). In several of these cases (US 2022, Mexico 2022-23) management first reported little elasticity and later conceded it. `[sourced]` call 2015-07-22 p.6; call 2019-07-23 p.7; call 2022-07-21 p.9-10; call 2022-10-27 p.4; call 2023-10-26 p.10.
- Since 2023 the stated approach is price-pack architecture, value packs and no price rises, i.e. volume defence (call 2023-04-27 p.7; call 2023-10-26 p.15), then disciplined pricing in 2026 (call 2026-04-29 p.5). `[sourced]`

### 2.2 Commodity pass-through
- Hedge window lengthened: ~6 months average (2011), 3-9 then 4-9 months (2011-2014), 4-12 months (Oct 2015), up to 18 months in 2020 and 4-18 months in 2022; this delays both cost increases and cost relief (call 2011-07-22 p.5; call 2011-10-28 p.6; call 2015-10-23 p.11; call 2020-04-27 p.10; call 2022-04-26 p.6). `[sourced]`
- Relief and pain lag by 6-12 months: lower wheat shows up first half 2020 (call 2019-05-02 p.12); 2022 Ukraine-era hedges hit 4Q22 (call 2022-10-27 p.5); 2023 wheat deflation reached margin in 4Q23 (call 2023-10-26 p.13); Mexico's 2025 margin was pressured by hedges struck 6-8 months before (call 2025-10-28 p.4, p.8). `[sourced]`
- Pass-through is partial and sized by no one: the company refused to quantify price rises in 2021 and 2022 (call 2021-07-28 p.9; call 2022-02-22 p.8) and offers no pass-through commitment for 2027 (call 2026-07-23 p.9). `[sourced]`
- Wheat is not the main driver on management's own numbers: flour ~9-10% of sales (call 2012-07-26 p.10), less than 1/4 of raw materials (call 2013-04-26 p.9), sugar <3% of cost of sales (call 2023-10-26 p.13); packaging, fats, labour, freight and FX matter. `[sourced]`

### 2.3 North America turnaround claims
- Three recovery dates: 2H24 (call 2024-02-19; call 2024-04-22 p.8, p.10), gradual improvement in 2H25 and benefits over 3-4 years (call 2025-04-29 p.5, p.7), 2 or 3 years more (call 2026-04-29 p.11). Earlier cycles: Sara Lee synergies by 2013 then 2014; US margin target 10% over time (call 2015-04-24 p.7); VSP payback under 2 years (call 2018-07-25 p.5). The vocabulary shifted from integration (2011-2014) to restructuring to VSP to transformation (2024) to productivity (2025-2026). `[sourced]`
- Facts behind it: see [[bimbo-north-america-turnaround-evidence]].

### 2.4 Europe and Asia, including the healthy-food trend
- ==No call in 2011-2Q26 discusses a European healthy-food trend directly.== Europe appears as: Iberia private-label price war (2012-2016, call 2012-10-26 p.4; call 2016-02-26 p.9); Panrico/Donuts Iberia integration with high-single-digit EBITDA margin promised in 30 months (call 2015-07-22 p.4); East Balt and QSR (2017); Iberia integration delays (2018-2019); private-label mix again in 2022-23 (call 2022-10-27 p.5; call 2023-02-22 p.5); and then Eastern Europe/Balkans/Mediterranean bolt-ons (Vel Pitar, Karamolegos, Don Don, Moulin d'Or, Bonel). `[sourced]`
- Health and wellness is a group-level theme, not a European one: whole grain and trans-fat removal (2012-2014), 133 reformulated products (deck 2014-05-01 p.15), Sanissimo (call 2015-02-27 p.4), No Added Nonsense and organic in the US (call 2019-10-25 p.4), 42% of sales with a healthy profile (call 2024-02-19 p.4), protein bagels, half loaves and premium bread in the US (call 2025-07-24 p.5; call 2025-04-29 p.9), nutrition ratings HSR ≥3.5 for ~48% of sales and artificial colours out by end 2026 (call 2026-02-25 p.5; call 2025-07-24 p.4), and GLP-1 responses (fibre, protein, smaller portions) (call 2026-02-25 p.9). In Europe specifically: gluten-free (Amaritta, Spain, call 2024-02-19 p.5), crustless bread (call 2020-02-21 p.5) and Portugal share leadership (call 2017-07-26 p.4). `[sourced]`
- EAA margin path: 2015 -2.5% LTM (deck 2015-10-23 p.13) to 7.0% FY22 (deck 2023-02-22 p.15) to 10.8% FY25 (deck 2026-06-10 p.22); mostly organic and accretive per management, but organic share refused (call 2024-10-29 p.9-10). `[sourced]`
- China: Bimbo branded business a turnaround (Feb 2024) and small, a year to keep building (call 2024-10-29 p.12); Mankattan acquired 2018 as #2 baker (call 2018-07-25 p.5). `[sourced]`

### 2.5 Mexico consumer
- Volume weak 2013-14 (price, IEPS tax, consumer), recovery 2015-17, slowdown 2019 (traditional channel, South, remittances), COVID channel split (retail up, convenience and vending down), price-led growth 2021-22, volume stress 2H23 after price rises, positive volume 2024, soft remittances and weak August in 2025, share gains in 2026. Management has repeatedly forecast gradual or soon recoveries: 3Q14 rebound failed, mid-single-digit return in 2013 failed, soon growing again (Oct 2023) was met in 2024. `[sourced]` call 2013-04-26 p.12; call 2014-10-24 p.7; call 2023-10-26 p.12.
- Regulation: IEPS 2014 (call 2014-02-21 p.8), labelling NOM-051 (Oct 2020, call 2020-07-27 p.10), price-restraint talks on 24 basic goods in 2022 (call 2022-04-26 p.8), proposed 5-day week (call 2023-07-25 p.13; declined to comment), Congress and VAT in 2013 (call 2013-07-24 p.8). `[sourced]`

### 2.6 Tariffs
- Raised twice. Feb 2017: asked about a US-Mexico trade war; exports ~5% of Mexican revenue; wheat can be sourced elsewhere (call 2017-02-24 p.9-10). Apr 2025: direct impact not material, Mexico exports mostly USMCA-compliant, guidance assumes no incremental tariff impact; the uncertainty is tariff-driven inflation hitting US consumers (call 2025-04-29 p.7). Afterwards tariffs appear only as the cause of early-2025 peso weakness (call 2025-10-28 p.7); none in 2024 beyond analyst mention, none in 2026 (replaced by Iran-conflict inflation, call 2026-04-29 p.8). `[sourced]` Existing context on USMCA review dates: [[bimbo-catalyst-calendar-facts]].

### 2.7 ESG
- Escalation of claims: wind farm and IPC index (2012), trans fats (2014), palm oil and COP21 (2015), RE100 and 100% recyclable packaging by 2025 (2018-19), net zero 2050 SBTi (2021), formal strategy day (May 2022), sustainability-linked RCF and MXN15bn bond (2021-23), artificial colours out by end 2026 (2025). Stated Scope 3 progress is thin: -12% vs 2022 but only -3.7% vs the 2019 baseline against targets of -12.5% by 2025 and -28% by 2030 (call 2024-07-24 p.4; deck 2023-07-11 p.23). The cost of net zero was not estimated at the May 2022 strategy day; spend this year ~US$2mm (call 2022-05-18 p.8). See [[bimbo-esg-framework]], [[bimbo-sustainable-financing]]. `[sourced]`

## 4. Connections and contradictions

- **Extends** [[bimbo-management-narrative-timeline]]: its phase D As anticipated (1Q25-2Q25) matches the cut-and-reassure pattern in the calls.
- **Refines the timing** in that article's phase C: it dates the first use of transformation for NA to the 1Q24 release; the 19 Feb 2024 call (4Q23) already announced a transformational program in North America to start in 2024 and called 2024 a transitional year (call 2024-02-19 p.4, p.6).

## Key Takeaways

- Tone tracks results with a lag: defensive 2011-13, sober 2014, steady 2015-18, least confident Jul 2019, peak 2020-21, cautious 2023-25, most confident Apr-Jul 2026 after an easy comp and one-offs.
- Price-first then elasticity-admission is a repeated cycle in Mexico and the US (2013-14, 2015, 2018-19, 2022-23); price-led growth has repeatedly been followed by volume loss (Mexico 2013-14, US 2015, US 2018-19, 2022-23); do not assume price carries volume.
- ==No call discusses a European healthy-food trend; Europe is a private-label, integration and bolt-on story with a rising margin (EAA 10.8% in FY25).== The healthy-food narrative is group-level and US-centred.
- Commodity pass-through is lagged (6-18 months), unsized and relied on productivity; 2027 hedges are placed at higher cost with no pricing commitment.
- Tariffs were dismissed as immaterial in 2025 and then dropped; the 2026 risk named by management is Iran-conflict inflation.
