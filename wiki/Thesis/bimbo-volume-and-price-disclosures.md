# Volume, price/mix, same-store, SKU and unit disclosures by region and quarter, 2011 to 2Q26

External evidence only. Nothing here is the team's view. Purpose: the mentor wants a volume x price forecast; this article lists every volume, price/mix, same-store, SKU, route, plant and category datapoint management gave on calls and decks, and states where **no figure exists**. Companions: [[bimbo-quarterly-trend-4q21-2q26]] (reported totals), [[bimbo-north-america-turnaround-evidence]], [[bimbo-segments-and-regions]], [[bimbo-input-costs]], [[bimbo-guidance-vs-delivery-2011-2026]], [[bimbo-call-themes-by-year]].

**Citation key.** `call yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = Capital IQ transcript (PDF page); `deck yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = company slides or release on that date; files in `Source`. MXN unless stated. ex-FX = constant currency as defined by management. A volume or price percentage appears only where management said it; everything else is flagged no figure.

> [!warning] Bottom line on data availability
> Management has **never published group or regional volume growth as a time series**. Numeric volume appears in 2011-2017 for Mexico and (rarely) the US, and almost never afterwards. From 2018 the disclosure is qualitative (volume strong, positive volume), and management has declined price/volume splits explicitly (call 2021-07-28 p.11; call 2022-02-22 p.8; call 2025-04-29 p.8). From Q3 2015 the commentary moved to cumulative framing (call 2015-10-23 p.3). The only multi-year quantity series in the files is the sustainability annex's tonnes of packaged product (section 6). `[sourced]`

## 7. What a volume x price forecast can and cannot use `[calc]`

- Volume cannot be reconstructed from sales growth and price because management gives neither. The nearest usable pieces: (a) Mexico volume history 2014-2017 (-6% to +4%); (b) US category tonnage (-2% in 2016, -2.1% to -2.3% in 2023); (c) group volume -1%+ in 2Q23; (d) tonnes of packaged product 2019-2025 (scope-affected); (e) the decomposition volume vs price about 50-50 (US 1Q16, 3Q16).
- ex-FX sales growth by region is available each quarter (Sections 1-4); with management's price statements (no price rise in the US in 2023; three rises in 2021-22; Mexico price in line with inflation) one can bound price/mix and infer volume as a residual. Treat the residual as an assumption, not a datapoint.
- Mexico: price below or equal to inflation since 2017 and positive volume from 4Q23, so sales growth of 3-5% implies low-to-mid single-digit volume only if price/mix is ~1-2% `[assumption]`; the company never confirmed it.
- North America: ex-FX sales declined in each of the eight quarters 1Q24-4Q25 on call data (+0.7% in 1Q26); the Jun 2026 deck series starts at 4Q23 and shows 4Q23 negative too (deck 2026-06-10 p.11), which conflicts with its own statement that 1Q26 was the first positive quarter since 4Q23 while price/mix turned positive (3Q25) and US volumes were still declining in 2025 (call 2026-02-25 p.13); so NA volume bottomed at best in 1H26 and no number has been given.

## Key Takeaways

- ==Volume is almost never quantified after 2017; management declined the price/volume split in 2021 and 2022 and gives only ex-FX sales growth by region==, so a volume x price forecast must rest on assumptions anchored to ex-FX sales and category data.
- Mexico: volume fell 3-6% in 2014 (tax and weak consumer), recovered to about +4% in 2015-2017, and was positive for four straight quarters by 3Q24; 2025 sales growth slowed to +0.3% in 3Q25 before +4.8% in 4Q25.
- North America: ex-FX sales fell in each of the eight quarters 1Q24-4Q25 (-4.5% to -3% on the calls) before +0.7% and +1.3% in 1H26; US volumes were still declining in 2025; price/mix, not volume, drove the recovery.
- Group volume: -1% in 2Q23, positive (no number) in 2024 and 2025; the sustainability annex shows packaged tonnes -7.8% in 2025 with scope caveats, a cross-check the team should run.
- Pass-through: hedge windows lengthened from ~6 months to 4-18 months, US price rises (2021-22) were followed by elasticity, private-label share gains and no 2023 price rise; for 2026-27 management gives inflation (US$70-90mm) but no pricing commitment.
