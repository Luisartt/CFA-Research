## Key Takeaways

- The filings give no future release date. History says 3Q results land 26 to 29 October and FY results 19 to 27 February; confirm the 3Q26 date outside these documents.
- Nearer-term debt calendar is light: Bimbo 23-2L (MXN 3,000 mm, 24 Jul 2026), a US$12.5 mm BofA instalment (11 Aug 2026) and Bimbo 17 (MXN 9,633 mm, 24 Sep 2027) before the 2028 and 2029 clusters (MXN 16.0 bn and 19.1 bn). Liquidity includes an undrawn US$2,350 mm revolver.
- Shareholder returns are annual and AGM-timed: dividend MXN 1.75 per share approved 30 Apr 2026 (up from 1.00 in 2025), paid from 13 May 2026; next AGM due by about end-April 2027.
- The NA turnaround has a dated trail: closures (4Q23 to 4Q24), distribution centre (2025), double-digit margin (3Q25), sales growth (1Q26), share gains (2Q26).
- The main external dated event cited is the July 2026 USMCA review; its result and any tariff changes after the filings' cut-off need a fresh source.
