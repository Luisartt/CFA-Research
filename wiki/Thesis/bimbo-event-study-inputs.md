# Event-Study Inputs for BIMBO A: Dated Events, Timing Rules and In-Document Price Evidence

A working table of dated events for a later share-price reaction analysis. ==No prices were fetched or computed from external series.== The only price information here is what the source documents themselves print (broker notes, news wraps), flagged as such. Facts and timing rules only; the choice of windows, benchmark and model belongs to the team. Events are derived from [[bimbo-press-release-timeline-2010-2026]] (dates and numbers), [[bimbo-sell-side-research-2014-2020]] (broker calls and prices), [[bimbo-analyst-forecast-accuracy]] (estimate surprises) and [[bimbo-sp-global-esg-score-history]] (ESG updates). Existing event lists: [[bimbo-catalyst-calendar-facts]], [[bimbo-management-narrative-timeline]].

**Conventions.** Dates are printed announcement dates (not CIQ file dates). "Reaction day" = first BMV trading day on which the news could be in the price. `[sourced]` = printed in the cited document; `[calc]` = my arithmetic; `[assumption]` = a rule I propose that the team should verify; `[unverified]` = cannot be established from the files. Time zones: Mexico City is UTC-6 (no daylight saving since Oct-2022; before that UTC-5 in summer) and the BMV session is about 08:30-15:00 local `[assumption]`; CIQ transcript folder times are US Eastern.

## 1. Timing rules found in the files

1. ==Results releases are after the market close.== From 1Q20 the release and the conference call are on the same date, with the call at 18:00-19:00 ET (17:00-18:00 Mexico City) `[sourced]` call folders 2020-04-27 to 2026-07-23 ("1800", "1900"; 2Q26 "1700" after a time revision from 18:00, `Bimbo_2026-07-21_PR`). Reaction day = next trading day `[assumption]`. Example in the files: the 1Q26 release and call were on 29-Apr-2026 and ==Bimbo rose 9.1% on 30-Apr-2026 while the IPC rose 1.1%== `[sourced]` News highlights, "Mexican Stocks Rise 1.1%", 30-Apr-2026.
2. 2011-2019 (call at 09:00-11:00 ET): the release is dated the evening before the call. Evidence: Interacciones' quick views carry the release date and the call is the next morning (release 26-Apr-2017 / call 27-Apr-2017; 22-Feb-2018 / 23-Feb-2018; 26-Oct-2017 / 27-Oct-2017) `[sourced]`. Reaction day = call date `[assumption]`; for quarters not checked against a printed release date the rule is `[unverified]`.
3. M&A and BMV "Eventos Relevantes" notices carry no time stamp in the CIQ copies. The Canada Bread M&A call was at 11:00 ET on 12-Feb-2014 (inside the session) so the first reaction day is 12-Feb-2014 or earlier `[sourced]` call folder; use [-1, +3] windows `[assumption]`.
4. CIQ file names are often one day earlier than the printed date; always use the date in the document ([[bimbo-press-release-timeline-2010-2026]] section 4).

## 8. Gaps and cautions

- Press-release time stamps are absent; only the 2026 BBU HQ release (12:36 ET) and the call times give intraday information.
- Prices in the broker notes are "as of" the note date and may be the prior close; they are not event-window prices.
- The "Mexican Stocks" wraps are a biased sample (top movers only).
- Fitch notices (Aug-2025 affirmation and short stubs) are unreadable or empty in the CIQ copies; rating events from Fitch are therefore listed only where S&P or the company release repeats them.
- Several 2019-2025 events overlap with results days; a clean event study of non-results news will have few usable observations (about 10 isolated corporate events 2014-2026 `[calc]` from the cluster table).

## Key Takeaways

- Results come out after the close (1Q20 onward the same evening as a 17:00-19:00 ET call; earlier the night before a morning call), so the reaction day is the next session, and the one clean in-file example is +9.1% on 30-Apr-2026 vs IPC +1.1%.
- 42 dated corporate events, 13 overlap clusters and 12 broker-event rows are logged; most corporate events (bond issues, regulatory approvals) have no price evidence in the files and low expected surprise.
- Highest-information events: Canada Bread (12-Feb-2014), the Aug-2014 equity offering, East Balt (Oct-2017), Ricolino (25-Apr-2022), the 20-Mar-2025 S&P outlook change, the CEO changes (Apr-2024, 6-Nov-2025) and the 22-Apr-2026 HQ move; several sit in clusters with results days.
- No file gives a usable price series; prices printed in notes are pre-release levels and one-day mover lists are selected.
- Missing dates (offering, East Balt signing) are the largest data risk for a 2014 and 2017 event study.
