# Leverage, deleveraging, buybacks, dividends and M&A pace: what management said, 2011 to 2Q26, and whether it did it

External evidence only (earnings calls, call decks, M&A calls). Nothing here is the team's view. Mentor test: deleveraging and disciplined M&A are claims to be checked against the ratio history. Companions: [[bimbo-guidance-vs-delivery-2011-2026]] (scored targets), [[bimbo-capital-allocation]], [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-leverage]], [[bimbo-capital-structure-and-debt]], [[bimbo-shareholder-returns]], [[bimbo-acquisitions]].

**Citation key.** `call yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = Capital IQ transcript (PDF page); `deck yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = company slides or release on that date; paths in `Source`. MXN unless stated.

- Management's own historical claim is proven track record of deleveraging (deck 2018-04-27 p.17; Proven Ability to Delever slide in 2023 decks). ==The ratio history shows a repeated cycle: lever up with a deal (Sara Lee 2011, Canada Bread 2014, East Balt 2017, capex plus bolt-ons 2022-24), then take 2-5 years to return to 2.5x, never to the stated <2x until the COVID years and the Ricolino sale.== `[calc]`
- Deleveraging came from three non-organic sources: Ricolino sale (EV MXN27bn, ~0.4-0.5x effect, call 2022-10-27 p.9); peso strength (a stronger peso lowers peso-translated US-dollar debt, e.g. USD debt revalued by stronger peso, call 2025-07-24 p.6); and capex under-spend (US$1.2bn in 2025 vs US$1.3-1.4bn guide, call 2026-02-25 p.6-7). `[sourced]`
- The 2025 improvement is flagged by the CFO as partly timing: 2026 capex carryover (call 2026-02-25 p.7). Capex was then cut again to US$1.0-1.2bn in July 2026 because projects are running behind schedule and evolving volume trends (call 2026-07-23 p.5, p.8). `[sourced]`

## Key Takeaways

- ==Management held its stated <2x leverage ceiling only from Dec 2020 to 3Q23 (and that window relied on COVID cash flow and the Ricolino sale)== `[calc]`; from 1.5x at Dec 2022 the ratio nearly doubled to 2.9x by Dec 2024 and is 2.5x at 2Q26 (call 2026-07-23 p.10).
- The metric and the target keep moving (total to net, pro forma, ex-VSP, ex-IFRS 16; <2x to 2.0-2.5x to around 2.5x); the CFO still says above the target zone at 2.5x-2.7x.
- 2025 deleveraging (2.9x to 2.7x) leaned on capex under-spend, FX and a halved buyback; capex was cut again for 2026, so FCF-led deleveraging in 2026 is partly deferred spend.
- Buybacks and dividends continued through every leverage rise (2018, 2020, 2024); the payout is not tied to any disclosed ratio.
- M&A pace: ~5-6 bolt-ons a year with undisclosed prices, after big deals at 8-9x EBITDA that took 3-5 years to digest; forecast any new deal as incremental leverage with a delay in the stated synergies.
