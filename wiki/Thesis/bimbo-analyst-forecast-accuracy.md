## 1. What can and cannot be tested

- ==No broker note in the corpus gives a volume forecast.== Volumes are discussed only in words ("good performance in both the US and Canadian markets", INT-141016 p.1). Revenue errors in section 2 are therefore mostly FX translation (peso moves 2014-2017), not volume. The company prints no regional volume percentages either [[bimbo-north-america-turnaround-evidence]].
- Margin forecasts can be tested (EBITDA and sales are printed), but only from Sep-2014. Nothing exists for Sara Lee (2010-11) from brokers other than INVEX's rating table; the Sara Lee test in section 5 therefore uses management's own claims.
- Interacciones changed its EBITDA definition between notes (2014 EBITDA appears as 19,275E, 18,675, 16,319, 16,267 and 18,418); annual comparisons use the filings' adjusted EBITDA (FY14 18,420; FY15 23,369; FY16 29,298; FY17 27,289; FY18 31,705) `[sourced]` [[bimbo-income-statement-fy14-fy20]].

Actual: 10.7%, 11.6%, 10.2%. At FY17 sales, 4.6 points of margin equals about MXN 12.3bn of EBITDA `[calc]` (267,515 x 4.6%).
- The 2015E forecast made in Apr-2015 (sales 212,067, EBITDA 21,884, EPS 1.11) was within 3-7% on the top line and EBITDA and 1% on EPS (1.11 vs 1.10): close forecasts exist, but the same analyst's Jul-2015 EPS estimate for 2015 (1.56) was 42% above the 1.10 reported `[calc]` (1.56 / 1.10 - 1), because the financing and tax lines moved.
- May-2018: the new analyst said he was "more conservative than management"; his FY18 EBITDA estimate ended 10% below the outcome and his sales estimate 3% below.

## 6. Patterns for the team to test against its own forecasts `[calc]` unless noted

1. Sales and EBITDA errors are small in a quarter (mean absolute 2% and 5%) and FX-driven; ==the dangerous line is net income== (mean absolute 34%): tax rate swings (43% to 84% in single quarters), FX on USD debt, pension charges.
2. Multi-year margin paths after acquisitions have been the real error: 14.8% expected for FY17 vs 10.2% reported. Compare any FY27-FY28 margin expansion thesis with the history in [[bimbo-income-statement-fy14-fy20]] and with current consensus drift in [[ciq-estimate-revisions-and-surprises]] (FY2026 EPS -21% over 18 months while targets rose `[sourced]` there).
3. Management's own deleveraging promises were met on time once (Canada Bread, 1.6 years) and reversed by the next deal; leverage commentary should be paired with the pipeline of deals.
4. Management's deal bases ("earnings capacity", "synergized EBITDA") ran 5% above the last reported year (Canada Bread) or relied on a profit base that fell 28% before closing (Sara Lee); the deals' integration benefits were never reported as a line.
5. Skeptical calls are not automatically right: the 2011-13 Sells lost; the 2013-15 Sells after rallies won (INVEX Sell calls: 5 of 9 won, [[bimbo-sell-side-research-2014-2020]]).

## Key Takeaways

- Across nine previews, Interacciones' EBITDA error averaged 4.9% in absolute terms but net income error averaged 34%; three quarters missed net income by 50% or more.
- The big failure was the multi-year margin path: Jul-2015 forecast of 14.8% EBITDA margin for FY17 vs 10.2% actual, after the 2014-16 acquisitions.
- Net income was over-forecast in 8 of 9 annual vintages while EBITDA was under-forecast in 7 of 9: translation and below-the-line items, not operations, drove the errors.
- Canada Bread: Bimbo's EBITDA base (CAD 185m) was about 5% above the seller's last reported year; deleveraging was achieved in about 1.6 years and reversed by 2017; a CAD 50m fine and a CAD 2bn lawsuit followed.
- Sara Lee: the target's EBITDA base fell from US$ 108m to US$ 78m before closing; synergies slipped a year and delivered about US$ 120m of the US$ 150-200m promised by 2013; the pro forma 11.1% margin was 7.3% in FY13, yet the stock rose, so a skeptic must separate "claims fail" from "price falls".
- No volume forecasts exist in the corpus; any volume thesis needs a source other than these notes.
