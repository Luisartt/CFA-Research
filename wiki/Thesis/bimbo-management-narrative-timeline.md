# What management said each quarter, 4Q21 to 2Q26

External/management evidence only. Source is the opening CEO and CFO statements (PDF p.1) and the "highlights" pages (p.2-3) of each earnings release, in Spanish; paraphrased here. No assessment of whether management was right. Links: [[bimbo-north-america-turnaround-evidence]] (the numbers behind the NA story), [[bimbo-catalyst-calendar-facts]], [[bimbo-quarterly-trend-4q21-2q26]], [[bimbo-guidance-2026]].

**Conventions.** Release dates are as printed on the cover (4T21: call date, 4T21 p.8). Speakers: Daniel Servitje (Chairman and CEO until 30 Apr 2024; Executive Chairman from 1 May 2024, 1T24 p.2); Rafael Pamias (CEO 1 May 2024 to Oct/Nov 2025); Alejandro Rodriguez Bas (CEO from November 2025, 4T25 p.2); Diego Gaxiola (Global CFO throughout). Group figures in MXN millions; "ex-FX" per management. Net debt / EBITDA excludes IFRS 16. Every figure `[sourced]` to the release named unless tagged otherwise.

## 2. Phases and shifts in tone

Phases are the compiler's grouping of the wording above; the quotes and numbers are management's.

| Phase | Quarters | Dominant wording | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Price-led growth through inflation | 4Q21-4Q22 | "Exceptional", record sales, volume growth "despite price increases", "right levers" against inflation | Offence: capex records, share gains, Ricolino sale, acquisitions; margins slightly down on raw materials (-120bp to -40bp YoY) |
| B. Record results, FX caveat | 1Q23-4Q23 | Record sales/EBITDA, pricing carry-over, "excluding the super peso" | Investment-grade and capex language rises; Moody's/S&P/Fitch upgrades (1T23 p.2) |

| D. "As anticipated" | 1Q25-2Q25 | Volatile and uncertain macro; margin contraction "as anticipated" | CFO repeatedly points back to what was pre-announced; confidence stated for the long term, not the quarter |
| E. Stabilisation to recovery | 3Q25-2Q26 | "Clear signs of stabilisation" (3Q25), "encouraging signs of improved demand" (1Q26), "tangible progress" (2Q26) | New CEO from Nov 2025; "record productivity benefits" replaces "investments"; deleveraging, free cash flow and shareholder returns move to the CFO message; consumer environment still called challenging in 2Q26 (2T26 p.1, p.3) |

## Key Takeaways

- Management's NA story has a visible sequence: "investment and transformation" (1Q24) → payoff dated to 2H25 (4Q24) → "as anticipated" margin pressure (1H25) → "stabilisation" (3Q25) → "tangible progress" (2Q26) `[sourced]`.
- Diversification (Mexico, EAA, LatAm) is the stated shield through NA weakness; its mentions peak in 2024 and 3Q25 and fade by 1Q26 `[calc]`.
- The CEO changed twice in the window (Pamias May 2024, Rodriguez Bas Nov 2025); the CFO has been constant, so financial-policy language (deleveraging, shareholder returns, investment grade) has stayed continuous.
- The releases give direction on guidance ("met", "exceeded", "commitments for the year") but no numbers; numeric guidance must come from other documents.
- Tone in 2Q26 is confident on execution and cautious on demand: share gains and margin expansion alongside "challenging consumer environment" and a World Cup tailwind described as smaller.
