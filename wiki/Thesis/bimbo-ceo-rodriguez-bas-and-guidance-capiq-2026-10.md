## Key Takeaways

- The CEO is Alejandro Rodríguez Bas (Barcel and PepsiCo background, Harvard MBA), effective November 2025; no stated reason for the change and no strategic pivot since.
- 2026 guidance was improved in July: margin expansion +70 to +120 bp and capex US$1.0-1.2bn, with leverage at 2.5x and results at or above guide in the last three quarters.
- Share-gain and "beat" claims are management's own; compensation, bonus metrics and ownership of executives are not public in the files.
- Bimbo has changed CEO twice in two years; its closest peers' CEOs have not changed.
- Do not use the output's "prior guidance" column (except the 2025 capex guide, confirmed by the wiki) or the Arca pay structure.
