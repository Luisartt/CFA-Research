External/management evidence only. Nothing here is the team's view. Companion articles: [[bimbo-management-narrative-timeline]], [[bimbo-catalyst-calendar-facts]]. Business context in [[bimbo-segments-and-regions]]; quarterly consolidated series in [[bimbo-quarterly-trend-4q21-2q26]].

**Conventions.** Quarters follow Bimbo's labels (1T = 1Q). "Release" = quarterly earnings release (Spanish). Page cites are PDF page numbers (e.g. "4T25 p.5"), not the printed footer number. MXN millions unless stated. "ex-FX" = management's "excluding exchange-rate effect" (translation of non-Mexican subsidiaries). North America (NA) = US + Canada (4T25 p.4 note 6). Adjusted EBITDA = "UAFIDA Ajustada" (before interest, tax, D&A, impairments and multi-employer pension plan items; 2T26 p.2 note 2). Regional results exclude intercompany royalties (2T26 p.4).

## 1. Why this region matters (scale)

- NA = ==42.6%== of last-twelve-month net sales to 30 Jun 2026 `[sourced]` 2T26 p.3. It was 44.6% at Dec 2025 (4T25 p.4) and 45.8% at Sep 2025 (3T25 p.3) `[sourced]`; the share falls as the peso translates NA lower and other regions grow.
- FY25 NA net sales MXN 190,211 mm (0.5% in MXN, -4.0% ex-FX); NA adjusted EBITDA 17,117 mm (9.0% margin) vs group 59,456 mm `[sourced]` 4T25 p.4, p.6-7. `[calc]` NA = 28.8% of group EBITDA (17,117 / 59,456), before the royalty/intercompany treatment noted above.
- FY23 NA EBITDA was 20,217 mm at a 10.5% margin `[sourced]` 4T24 p.6-7; FY24 fell to 15,813 mm / 8.4% (-21.8% in MXN, -23.9% ex-FX) `[sourced]` 4T24 p.6. That 2023-to-2024 step-down is the starting point of the "turnaround" evidence below.

## 4. Management's stated NA initiatives (chronology)

All `[sourced]`; wording paraphrased.

- **4Q23 (release 19 Feb 2024):** NA margin -130bp partly from restructuring investments, "including the closure of a bakery in Albuquerque, US" (4T23 p.7). Other drivers: peso appreciation raising the cost of product imported from Mexico, inflation in labour.
- **1Q24 (22 Apr 2024):** Executive Chairman Daniel Servitje frames 2024 as "a year of investment and transformation", investing in NA for future growth (1T24 p.1). NA margin -160bp on peso strength, weak volumes, inflation and "transformation investments throughout the value chain" (1T24 p.5).
- **2Q24 (24 Jul 2024):** CEO Rafael Pamias cites "strategic investments in innovation and transformation projects" for long-term value (2T24 p.1). NA margin -130bp: weak volumes, transformation investments, peso effect on imported product, inflation, offset by lower raw materials and productivity (2T24 p.5).
- **3Q24 (29 Oct 2024):** Operating income note: value-chain investments in NA "to obtain long-term benefits" and ==closure of three bakeries in the US "to maximise the industrial footprint"==; closures of one bakery in Canada and one in Spain also announced (3T24 p.5). NA margin -140bp (3T24 p.5).
- **4Q24 (27 Feb 2025):** NA margin -390bp: weak sales, strategic value-chain investments "to grow capacities and better serve more customers and consumers", and the impact of the ==four previously announced bakery closures==; partly offset by lower raw materials and "productivity benefits from the initial investments in the project" (4T24 p.7). FY24: closure of 11 bakeries worldwide (4T24 p.6; also RA25 p.76-77, informe anual 2024 p.10). CEO says NA is a project "expected to gradually deliver benefits in the second half of 2025" (4T24 p.1). CFO: investing in "our largest operation" for sustainable long-term growth (4T24 p.1).
- **1Q25 (29 Apr 2025):** NA margin -130bp from weak sales and the continuing "long-term value chain investment programme" to increase capacity; offset by lower raw materials and productivity benefits from initial project investments and past restructuring (1T25 p.5). CFO: margin contraction "as anticipated" (1T25 p.1).
- **2Q25 (24 Jul 2025):** NA margin 9.0% (-70bp), ==sequentially up from 5.9% in 4Q24 and 7.4% in 1Q25==; "expected solid productivity benefits" from the transformation investments "continue to materialise", plus lower raw materials (2T25 p.5). CEO: "significant advances in productivity" and share recovered in key categories (2T25 p.1).
- **3Q25 (28 Oct 2025):** NA margin 10.4% (+90bp), "return to double-digit" EBITDA margin, three consecutive sequential improvements; drivers: lower raw materials and "record productivity benefits" from the transformation programme (3T25 p.2, p.5).
- **4Q25 (25 Feb 2026):** NA margin +330bp to 9.2%: lower raw materials, better price/mix, record productivity benefits, lower restructuring investment (4T25 p.7). FY25 NA margin +60bp to 9.0% (4T25 p.2). Operating income: record productivity benefits and lower restructuring spend (4T25 p.6).
- **1Q26 (29 Apr 2026):** NA margin +120bp to 8.6%: record productivity benefits from the transformation project, better sales, lower raw materials, lower restructuring investment (1T26 p.6).
- **2Q26 (23 Jul 2026):** NA margin +100bp to 10.0% "double-digit": record productivity benefits plus sales helped partly by the football World Cup (2T26 p.5). CEO: "tangible progress" of NA transformation initiatives (2T26 p.1). Group operating margin +50bp to 8.4% "largely" on the record NA productivity benefits (2T26 p.5).

Physical footprint facts:

- 2025: 29 bakeries/plants added to the manufacturing footprint, mostly via acquisitions; three closed "as part of optimising our global presence" (all regions); a new distribution centre opened in North Aurora, Illinois, the third in NA, to improve logistics network efficiency and resilience `[sourced]` Celebramos p.10; RA25 p.77. Capex ~US$1,200 mm in 2025 `[sourced]` 4T25 p.2.
- Capex was ~US$1,600 mm in 2024 `[sourced]` 4T24 p.2 and above US$2,000 mm in 2023 `[sourced]` 4T23 p.2 (all regions).
- Leadership: Alejandro Rodriguez Bas became Group CEO from November 2025 (4T25 p.2); a new President of Bimbo Bakeries USA (BBU), Mr. Koehrsen, was named in January 2026 (RA25 p.180) `[sourced]`.

## Key Takeaways

- Evidence of a bottom: NA EBITDA margin hit ==5.9% in 4Q24== (FY24 8.4%, from 10.5% in FY23) and has expanded YoY for four straight quarters to 10.0% in 2Q26 `[sourced]`/`[calc]`.
- NA ex-FX net sales only turned positive in 1Q26 (+0.7%) and 2Q26 (+1.3%) after eight negative quarters; the recovery so far is margin-led, not volume-led, and volumes are not quantified in the releases.
- Management attributes the margin recovery to "record productivity benefits" from the transformation, lower raw materials and lower restructuring spend; it attributes the 2024-1H25 pressure to value-chain investment, 11 bakery closures worldwide, peso strength and weak consumption.
- Share claims are management's own: US share gains in all categories in 2Q26 (first since 2020). No third-party data in the sources.
- No program-level quantification (capex, savings, completion date) is disclosed in these documents; this is the main evidence gap.
- Consolidated prior-year figures were restated by small amounts between first print and later comparatives; NA regional lines checked were unchanged.
