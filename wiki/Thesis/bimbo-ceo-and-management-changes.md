## 3. Track record of the new CEO (Alejandro Rodriguez Bas)

What the calls say about him:
- He opens the Feb 2026 call by thanking Pamias, promises to build on this trajectory, growth through brands and global presence, and to preserve the culture; no new strategy (call 2026-02-25 p.4). `[sourced]`
- He describes himself as having been the leading person of salty snacks globally (call 2026-02-25 p.14); the steering-committee slides list him as Barcel president before he moved up (deck 2023-07-11 p.27: Rodriguez (Barcel)). `[sourced]` The annual-report summary (joined Oct 2021 as Barcel Global CEO; ex-PepsiCo, Lala, C&S, Acosta) sits in [[bimbo-governance-and-ownership]] `[sourced]` RA25 p.178 per that article.
- He says he knows Barcel well and would like Barcel chips in the US one day (call 2026-04-29 p.10); no plan disclosed.
- His stated position on cost inflation for 2027: look inward; the transformation going to take 2 or 3 years (call 2026-04-29 p.11); pricing is disciplined, the focus is productivity (call 2026-07-23 p.9). `[sourced]`

- Scale of the business: Pamias called Bimbo a very small player globally in salty snacks in Oct 2023 (call 2023-10-26 p.14); the Feb 2026 deck ranks group snacks as a top-5 global player with 1.4% share (1.6% in the Jun 2026 deck), up from a top-10 position and ~1% before (deck 2026-02-27 p.6; deck 2026-06-10 p.5). `[sourced]`

The strong category data is the company's selection of one 4-week and one 12-week window. `[sourced]`
- Salty snacks double-digit in Q4 2025, per an analyst (call 2026-02-25 p.14). `[sourced]` secondhand.

## Key Takeaways

- ==Two CEO changes in about 18 months (May 2024 and Nov 2025) after the long Servitje era (43 years at the company), with the second exit unexplained in every file read== `[calc]`; the Executive Chair stays and the CFO has been constant since 2017.
- The new CEO's evidenced credential is salty snacks (Barcel/Takis), a business the company will not size; US salty-snack share data are favourable but the company chose the windows.
- The post-Nov-2025 improvement (NA margin, guidance raises, deleveraging) rests on easy comps, one-offs, FX and a cost program Koehrsen designed; attribution to the CEO is not supported by the files.
- Predecessor pattern: each transition followed a weak stretch (Brazil 2012-13, US 2014-15, NA 2023-24); the transformation timeline for NA has been reset with each leader (3-4 years Apr 2025; 2-3 more years Apr 2026).
- Disclosure-quality flags to track under the new team: preliminary transcript, misquoted margin delta, unexplained eliminations line, no regional margin guidance.
