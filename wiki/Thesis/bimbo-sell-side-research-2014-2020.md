## Key Takeaways

- Three brokers plus S&P ESG: 58 files, about 40 distinct documents (24 Interacciones, 4 Ve por Mas, 5 INVEX, 6 S&P ESG, 1 spreadsheet); only Interacciones and INVEX issued ratings. ==Interacciones kept a 44 target for nearly 46 months and a Hold for the last 31 of them==; its one Buy (Sep-2014 at 39.72) worked (+19.7% to 47.55) but on a 30x P/E applied to EPS that proved 34% too high.
- INVEX's directional Buy/Sell calls were right 8 of 14 times over ~90-day windows; its Sell calls on the Sara Lee integration (2011-13) lost, its Sells after rallies (2013, 2014, 2015) worked.
- Management-style claims repeated by brokers (deleveraging to 2x, acquisitions slowing, margins to 12-15%) were wrong or unmet within 2-3 years; leverage and deals went the other way.
- Brokers consistently miss on below-EBITDA items (tax, FX, pension charges); see [[bimbo-analyst-forecast-accuracy]].
- Data hygiene matters: one misfiled report, one stale preview, several sign and label errors; check before quoting any single cell.
- No broker research exists in the corpus for 2018-05 to 2020-02 or after 2020-08, and none before 2014: the team needs another source for those windows.
