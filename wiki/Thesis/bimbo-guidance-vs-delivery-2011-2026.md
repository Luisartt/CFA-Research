# Guidance versus delivery, 2011 to 2Q26: every explicit management target tested against what was reported next

External evidence only (earnings calls, call slides and investor decks from CapIQ). Nothing here is the team's view. Mentor frame: management's narrative is a claim to test. Companion articles: [[bimbo-management-narrative-timeline]] (what the releases said each quarter), [[bimbo-guidance-2026]] (2026 statements in releases), [[bimbo-leverage-and-deleveraging-commentary]], [[bimbo-call-themes-by-year]], [[bimbo-ceo-and-management-changes]], [[bimbo-volume-and-price-disclosures]].

**Citation key.** `call yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = S&P Capital IQ transcript of that earnings call (PDF page N, page 1 is the CapIQ cover); `deck yyyy-mm-dd p.N` = company slide deck or press release filed with that call (slide number = PDF page unless noted); `release` = company press release. Files sit in `filings/capitaliq/News, Events and filings/Transcripts & Investor Presentations/<folder starting with the date>/` and `.../Recent Documents/Transcript & Presentation/Bimbo_<date>_*/`; the full list is in the `Source` field. The Recent Documents `TRANSCRIPT` files duplicate the first folder and were not double-counted. Currency MXN unless stated; `ex-FX` = management's constant-currency figure.

**Method.** A target is counted only when management gave a number, range, date or direction for a stated period and a later document reports the result. Met = result inside or better than the range; Missed = outside it to the downside (capex below range counts as Missed because it is a deviation from the promise, not a judgement of value); Mixed = depends on basis, partly met, or the target was redefined. Pending 2026 items are excluded. Verdicts are the compiler's `[calc]`.

> [!warning] Guidance regime changed in Nov 2019
> Before the Nov 2019 investor day, management repeatedly declined numeric guidance (we don't give guidance: call 2019-02-22 p.12; call 2016-10-28 p.9; call 2015-04-24 p.10; US margin guidance declined, call 2014-02-21 p.11). The first full-year sales/EBITDA/tax/capex guide in the files is the 2020 guide on deck 2019-11-18 p.187. Everything before that is a verbal or slide target, not formal guidance. `[sourced]`

From 2023 the pattern reverses: FY2023 cut (Jul 2023, call 2023-07-25 p.5-6), FY2024 cut (Apr 2024, call 2024-04-22 p.6), FY2025 cut twice (call 2025-04-29 p.6; call 2025-07-24 p.6), each time with FX named as the main driver (2025: 100% FX per the CFO, call 2025-07-24 p.9). Margin guides were met in 2023 and 2025 and missed in 2022 and 2024.

## 3. Patterns that matter for a forecast

- **Timing slips, size does not.** Closings and integrations are delivered, but late and more expensively: Sara Lee costs (US$120mm planned, US$120mm in 2012 alone), Canada ERP (US$10-15mm said, CAD40mm booked), Panrico (4Q15 said, Jul 2016 closed), Donuts Iberia (mostly 2017, into 2018), Wickbold synergies gradually (call 2026-07-23 p.10). `[sourced]`
- **Deleveraging targets were stated, then dropped or redefined.** <2x was on every 2014 deck (deck 2014-05-01 p.13) and disappears from the October 2014 deck (deck 2014-10-24 p.16); the leverage metric changed from total to net debt in Oct 2019 (deck 2019-10-25 p.11) and to ex-IFRS 16 net debt thereafter. See [[bimbo-leverage-and-deleveraging-commentary]].
- **Capex plans are routinely cut, but 2022-2023 ran the other way.** 2018, 2019, 2020, 2024 and 2025 came in below the first guide; 2022 and 2023 rose to record levels (US$1.4bn, US$2.0bn). Management now labels capex normalized (deck 2026-07-23 p.21), after earlier decks said peak investments completed (deck 2025-03-31 p.12). `[sourced]`
- **Tax-rate guides have been poor.** Four different 2017 numbers; FY14 42.5% vs ~40%. Tax guidance from 2022 has been closer (33.5% vs low-to-mid 30s). `[sourced]`
- **Not the whole story in the beats.** The 2020-22 beats coincide with peak North America margin (12.9% FY20, 12.2% FY21; deck 2026-02-27 p.12) which then reversed: 11.0% (2022), 10.5% (2023), 8.4% (2024). The guidance record says nothing about NA margin because management refuses to give a regional margin guide (call 2026-02-25 p.11; call 2026-04-29 p.11). `[sourced]`
- **FX is the standing explanation for cuts.** FX was the stated cause of the 2023, 2024 and 2025 cuts (call 2023-07-25 p.5-6; call 2024-04-22 p.6; call 2025-07-24 p.9). Management quantifies FX in points of sales growth (2.0-2.5pp per 0.5-0.75 MXN move, call 2025-04-29 p.5; call 2025-07-24 p.6; 2026 guide: more than 500-600 bps drag, deck 2026-04-29 p.17). `[sourced]`

## Key Takeaways

- ==Of 61 testable targets, 22 were met (36%), 9 were mixed and 30 missed; excluding capex the met share is 40%== `[calc]`. Pre-2017 operating, integration and leverage targets mostly missed; post-2020 financial guides mostly beat.
- The post-2020 beats were set during COVID/inflation upswings and were followed by cuts in 2023, 2024 and 2025, each blamed mainly on FX and North America consumption (call 2025-07-24 p.6, p.9).
- Leverage promises slipped: <2x never held until 2020 (1.9x) and the target language was removed in 2014 and redefined in 2019. See [[bimbo-leverage-and-deleveraging-commentary]].
- Integrations arrive late and at higher cost (Sara Lee, Canada Bread EBITDA 5% below the valuation basis, Panrico, Donuts Iberia, Wickbold): apply a delay haircut to synergy timing in the forecast.
- NA recovery has been announced three times (2H24, 2H25, 2026) and delivered mainly as margin via cost cuts, not volume; the 2026 margin guide (+70-120bp) is therefore the most testable claim and the one with a ~50bp one-off in 1Q26.
