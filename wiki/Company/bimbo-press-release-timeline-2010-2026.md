## Key Takeaways

- 10 named acquisitions and about four bolt-ons in 2014-2026 against one large disposal (Ricolino, US$ 1.37bn): the company has been a serial acquirer, and every deal-multiple disclosed in the set sits at 7.7x-9.3x EV/EBITDA.
- Pre-sale Canada Bread was tidied (3 bakery closures, 2 disposals, a CAD 8.00 special dividend) before Bimbo paid CAD 1.83bn; the legacy cost came back as a CAD 50m fine (2023) and a CAD 2bn+ lawsuit (2024).
- Debt funding has moved to more expensive coupons: MXN 10-year fixed from 8.18% (2017) to 9.24% (2023); S&P put the BBB+ rating on negative outlook in Mar-2025 citing capex, dividends, buybacks and acquisitions above cash flow.
- Leadership: two CEO changes in 18 months (May-2024, Nov-2025), the second with a stated health and personal reason; CFO unchanged since 2017.
- Operating events in the press-release set are thin on plants, labor and recalls (one recall, one voluntary separation, one union contract, one HQ move): use the quarterly releases for closures and the NA transformation.
- Share count is shrinking slowly: -0.23% in 4.4 months of 2026 on BMV snapshots.
