# Segments and regions: North America, Mexico, EAA, Latin America

**Citation key.** `RA25`/`RA24`/`RA23`/`RA22`/`RA21` = annual reports to BMV for that fiscal year; `IA25-EEFF` = audited FY25 statements; `1T26`, `2T26`, `4T25` = earnings releases. `p.` = PDF page index. MXN millions. IFRS. Latest data: 2Q26 (period ended 2026-06-30, released 2026-07-23). "LTM 2Q26" = FY25 + 1Q26 + 2Q26 - 1Q25 - 2Q25 [calc].

## 1. How the company reports segments

- Four reportable geographic segments: **Mexico, North America (US + Canada), Latin America (Central and South America) and EAA (Europe, Asia, Africa)** [sourced] IA25-EEFF p.85 (note 26).
- Management groups operating segments by similar customers, products, production/distribution processes, regulation, inflation and currency trends [sourced] IA25-EEFF p.85.
- Regional EBITDA and operating income in the segment note and releases **exclude intercompany royalties**; consolidated figures eliminate intercompany [sourced] 4T25 p.6-7; IA25-EEFF p.85 footnote (1).
- Regional margins in releases are computed on **segment sales before eliminations** [calc] e.g. FY25 North America 17,117/190,211 = 9.0%, ties to 4T25 p.7 (9.0%).
- Business units inside the segments [sourced] RA25 p.83-87: North America = Bimbo Bakeries USA (BBU), Barcel USA, Bimbo Canada, plus QSR bakeries; Mexico = Bimbo and Barcel; Latin America = 15 countries; EAA = Iberia, UK, Romania and other Europe, China, India, Africa, plus Bimbo QSR outside the US.

> [!question] Where Bimbo QSR is reported is not stated in one place: the plants table lists 4 QSR bakeries under North America (RA25 p.115) while the release attributes EAA growth to "Bimbo QSR" (4T25 p.5). Confirm the segment mapping of QSR before building a segment model.

## 7. Segment profiles (what is in each, as described in the filings)

**North America (44.6% of FY25 sales, 28.8% of Adj. EBITDA)** [sourced] RA25 p.83-84, p.110
- US: BBU is headquartered in Dallas, reaches 50 states and 92% of US households (Circana); named the top supplier of baked goods in the US by Circana in 2025; 54 bakeries. Estimated 7.4% share of US bakery (Global Data, 2024). Barcel USA is the sixth-largest US salty-snack maker by sales (Circana), producing locally and importing from Mexico. Bimbo QSR is based in Ohio.
- Canada: Bimbo Canada (ex Canada Bread, acquired 2014), 17 bakeries, mostly independent distributors; brands include Dempster's, Villaggio, POM, Vachon; ~13.3% share of Canadian bakery (Global Data, 2024).
- Transformation programme: record productivity benefits, fewer restructuring costs and margin expansion in FY25 and 2026 [sourced] 4T25 p.2; 2T26 p.2. In 2024 the segment's EBITDA fell 21.8% on value-chain investments and exits from some private-label business [sourced] RA25 p.135, p.137; North America FY25 volumes were pressured by a weak industry consumption backdrop, with share gains in sweet baked goods, snacks and mainstream bread [sourced] 4T25 p.3. In 2Q26 the group says North America gained market share in all US categories, "something that had not happened since 2020" [sourced] 2T26 p.3.

**Mexico (32.4% of FY25 sales, ~55% of Adj. EBITDA incl. eliminations)** [sourced] RA25 p.84-85, p.111
- Bimbo (bread, sweet bakery, cookies, tortillas, bars) and Barcel (salty snacks; #2 in Mexico with Takis, Chips, Big Mix). Operates all routes directly; 38 plants. Listed as #1 in packaged bread and cakes and #2 in cookies by Nielsen.
- FY25 EBITDA margin 20.4%, a record; growth across all channels, with convenience double-digit [sourced] 4T25 p.2-4.

**Latin America (10.3% of FY25 sales)** [sourced] RA25 p.85, p.111
- 15 countries, 42 plants, leader in packaged bread in each country where it operates; EBITDA is >2.5x its 2020 level per the company [sourced] RA25 p.85. Operates in Argentina and Venezuela, both hyperinflationary under IFRS; Venezuela is no longer consolidated and is held as an equity instrument at fair value [sourced] RA25 p.85.
- Wickbold (Brazil) acquired October 2025; integration costs compress margins in 4Q25-2Q26 [sourced] 4T25 p.7-8; 2T26 p.5.

**EAA (12.7% of FY25 sales, 9.8% of Adj. EBITDA)** [sourced] RA25 p.85-87
- Europe: Iberia leader since 2011 (10 plants Spain, 2 Portugal); UK (New York Bakery bagels, St. Pierre brioche); Romania (Vel Pitar leader), plus Balkans (Don Don 2025), Turkey (30% UNO), Switzerland, Italy, France. Asia: China (3.0% share, #1 in packaged bread per GlobalData 2024) and India (Harvest Gold, Modern, Kitty). Africa: Morocco, Tunisia, South Africa (5 bakeries). Bimbo QSR supplies fast-food chains in ~40 countries.
- FY25 EBITDA margin 10.8%, a company record for the segment [sourced] 4T25 p.2. Brand-led business in China is weak in 2026 [sourced] 2T26 p.4.

## 8. Reading the segment numbers (technical notes)

- Peso translation matters. North America is ~43-45% of sales; the peso appreciated in 2025 and the first half of 2026 [sourced] 4T25 p.9; 2T26 p.3, so MXN segment sales and EBITDA can fall while constant-currency performance improves (see [[bimbo-fx-exposure]]).
- Earnings releases provide margin trend; the audited note provides the full segment bridge. Prefer the audited note for modelling (`data/financials.csv`) and the releases for quarterly drivers ([[bimbo-quarterly-trend-4q21-2q26]]).
- The "eliminations / other" line (FY25: +1,327) reconciles the sum of the regions to consolidated Adj. EBITDA; regional results exclude intercompany royalties and consolidated results eliminate intercompany items, but the filing does not itemise what the line contains [sourced] IA25-EEFF p.85; 4T25 p.6-7.

## Key Takeaways

- Mix is shifting away from North America (52% of sales in FY21 to 44.6% in FY25, 42.6% LTM 2Q26) toward Mexico (29% to 33%), EAA and Latin America, mostly via acquisitions and peso translation [sourced] RA22 p.220; 4T25 p.4; 2T26 p.3.
- Mexico carries the group's profitability: ~55% of FY25 Adj. EBITDA on ~32% of sales, margin 20.4% vs 9.0% in North America [sourced] Celebramos p.17; [calc] 4T25 p.7.
- North America's EBITDA margin fell from 12.2% (FY21) to 8.4% (FY24) and recovered to 9.0% (FY25) and 10.0% in 2Q26 [calc] and [sourced] 2T26 p.5; this is the single biggest swing factor in consolidated margin.
- EAA is the growth segment (EBITDA 2,704 in FY21 to 6,244 LTM 2Q26, margin 7.9% to 11.4%) [calc]; Latin America margin is compressed by Wickbold integration (LTM 7.6% vs 9.6% in FY23).
- The 1Q26 Mexico margin includes a one-off gain on an associate stake; strip it before using LTM Mexico margin.
- Data gaps: exact headcount by region; regional split of QSR; FY25 and FY24 segment capex lines need PDF verification.
