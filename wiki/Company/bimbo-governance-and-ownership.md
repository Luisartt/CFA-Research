## 6. Compensation

- Directors are paid per attended session: MXN 170,000 (in Mexico) or MXN 550,000 (outside Mexico, for directors residing elsewhere); Audit Committee MXN 150,000; Finance and Evaluation committees MXN 85,000. Executives who are directors are not paid. Total director/committee compensation in 2025: ~MXN 31.9 million [sourced] RA25 p.180.
- Key management compensation: 922 (FY25), 894 (FY24), 796 (FY23) MXN millions [sourced] RA25 p.181.
- Executive pay = fixed + variable (annual business plan across profitability, transformation, growth, people/reputation, with ESG indicators) + **virtual share plan (VEAB, since 2013)**: virtual shares allotted by level, salary and unit results, paid 30 months later in cash at the June average BMV price [sourced] RA25 p.181. A long-term cash bonus to certain executives is paid 30 months after grant [sourced] RA25 p.245.

## 8. Shareholder rights and corporate-action thresholds

- Holders of 10% of voting capital can call a general meeting, appoint one director, and request postponement of resolutions; 20% can challenge resolutions in court [sourced] RA25 p.191, p.198 (Mexican securities law).
- Ordinary AGM matters include approving the annual financials, dividend decisions, director election, the Audit Committee chair, the buy-back ceiling, and transactions of 20% or more of consolidated assets in a year; extraordinary AGM matters include capital changes, bylaw changes (including change-of-control clauses) and delisting [sourced] RA25 p.196.
- Share-buy-back reserve approved: 15,200 MXN millions at nominal value [sourced] RA25 p.309 (see [[bimbo-capital-allocation]]).
- Debt documents contain change-of-control events that trigger a repurchase offer where a change of control leads to a rating downgrade [sourced] RA25 p.6-7 (see [[bimbo-capital-structure-and-debt]]).

## Key Takeaways

- Control is concentrated: six holders own 72.03% of the shares (Normaciel 40.96%, Monser 12.78%), unchanged in absolute share count since 2023; their stake rose from 69.3% to 72.0% only because repurchased shares were cancelled [calc] RA22-RA25.
- The Executive Chairman (Daniel Servitje) is flagged by the company as influencing management and potentially holding "command power"; the CEO is now Alejandro Rodriguez Bas (since November 2025), the second CEO change since Servitje Montull stepped back in May 2024 [sourced] RA25 p.76-77, p.182.
- Board is large (18), 39% independent, 28% women, with 7 members carrying the Servitje surname; the three committees are chaired by Vallejo (audit), L. Jorba (compensation) and Rebolledo (finance); only the audit committee is fully independent [sourced] RA25 p.169, p.174-176.
- Related-party exposure is small relative to size: payables 1,154 (FY25) and mostly ordinary-course purchases from associates [sourced] RA25 p.274.
- Open items: ultimate beneficial owners of the holding companies; capital stock presentation (1,737 vs 3,866); fate of the prior CEO; the 2026 dividend per-share inconsistency in [[bimbo-capital-allocation]].
