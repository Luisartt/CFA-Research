## Key Takeaways

- The 15 largest institutional holders own only ≈4.9% of Bimbo; control (72.03% for the top block, Normaciel 40.96%) must be taken from the register, not from this data.
- Bimbo disclosed values for few deals: Trei Brutari (US$108.9m, 2.82x sales), Lender Bagel (US$33.2m), Blue Label (US$11.5m); Wickbold and the Nutresa stake have no price.
- The Nutrella sale and Bimbo insider trades are not in the data; Ricolino's wiki EV (Ps. 27bn, 2.66x sales) stays.
- Sector deals were financed with debt: Smucker-Hostess US$5.0bn, Flowers-Simple Mills at 3.1x-3.3x pro forma; Mars-Kellanova set 16.4x EBITDA as a reference.
- Peer controlling holders (Gruma, Arca, Lala) remain unverified.
