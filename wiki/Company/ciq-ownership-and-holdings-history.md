# Grupo Bimbo — Ownership and Holdings History (Capital IQ, 2004-2026)

The ownership files re-exported on 10-Oct-2026 contain two things the 06-Oct-2026 files used by [[ciq-ownership-structure]] did not: ==a full-history Ownership History sheet (annual positions of 437 holders at 31-Dec from 2004 to 2025, plus "Latest")== and refreshed Detailed and Cross-holdings snapshots priced at MXN 55.92 instead of 55.41. This article builds the time series, shows that CIQ did carry the controlling Servitje-family holding companies until 31-Dec-2024 (which changes how the earlier "CIQ misses the controlling shareholders" warning should be read), and lists what moved between 6 and 10 October. Filing-based view: [[bimbo-governance-and-ownership]]; shareholder returns: [[bimbo-shareholder-returns]].

**Citation key.** `CIQ-OH` = Ownership History sheet (10-Oct-2026; "Common Stock Equivalent Held", Period View Annual, Date Range All History; columns 12/31/2004 to 12/31/2025 and "Latest"). `CIQ-OD` = Ownership Detailed (latest filings per holder). `CIQ-BS` = Templated balance sheet (CIQ common shares outstanding at each FQ4, split-adjusted: 4,703m in 2004-2015, 4,305m at Dec-2025). Shares in millions (m); percentages of shares out use the CIQ-BS count of the same year `[calc]`. ## Key Takeaways

- ==CIQ carried the six family holding companies from 2007 through 31-Dec-2024 (2,929m shares, 67.7% of CIQ shares; 72.03% with the bank trustee per the register) and dropped them in the 2025 refresh==; the "94.5% public" reading in the 6-Oct/10-Oct snapshots is a data-feed artifact.
- Listed institutions held 2.9% (2004), 7.6% (2021) and 5.5% (Dec-2025) of the shares; the top five are 76% of that, led by BlackRock (104.1m, 2.42%).
- The Vanguard line fell 39% in 2025 but is an entity relabelling; Norges Bank, Impulsora de Fondos Banamex and GAMCO cut positions by 70%-100% over their histories.
- Between 6 and 10 October the price rose to MXN 55.92 (market cap 240,248) and the holder ranking changed (Deutsche 5th, Schwab 6th); BlackRock 104,061,882, Dimensional 11,969,309.
- FY2010 CIQ holdings are 4x overstated (unadjusted 4-for-1 split of 29-Apr-2011); the core family holders have not reduced their positions since 2009 and the block's percentage rises only because buybacks shrink the base.
