MXN millions unless stated. IFRS. Latest data point: 2Q26 (period ended 2026-06-30, released 2026-07-23). No target, rating or recommendation is implied.

## 1. Stated financial policy

The company describes its financial policy as [sourced] RA25 p.81-82: (a) a strong, resilient balance sheet; (b) efficient working capital; (c) flexibility and significant liquidity backed by a **US$2,350 million committed revolver**; (d) a reinvestment focus that prioritises growth and long-term profitability; (e) conservative risk-management guidelines. Ratings are cited as BBB+ (S&P and Fitch) and Baa1 (Moody's), described as the best-rated among global peers [sourced] RA25 p.82.

- No numeric dividend payout, leverage target or buy-back ceiling in pesos per year is stated in the annual report or releases reviewed. What exists: a shareholder-approved **buy-back reserve of 15,200** (nominal value) [sourced] RA25 p.309, and the dividend practice described in §4.
- The CFO frames FY25 as having "advanced" the "gradual deleveraging phase": net debt/Adj. EBITDA 2.7x, down 0.2x [sourced] 4T25 p.1-2. Deleveraging continued to 2.5x at 30 June 2026 [sourced] 2T26 p.1-2, p.6.

## 3. M&A policy and spending

## 7. Observed ordering of uses of cash (descriptive only)

FY21-FY22: high capex plus M&A out of cash and operating flow, supported by the 25,797 Ricolino proceeds (FY22), low leverage (1.5x at Dec-22). FY23-FY24: peak capex (US$2.0bn and US$1.6bn), moderate M&A, rising buybacks, leverage up to 2.9x. FY25: capex down ~23% in MXN, M&A up (Wickbold, Don Don), buybacks cut to 1,253, leverage down to 2.7x [calc from the tables above]. 1H26: leverage 2.5x, FCF ~19,000 combined [calc] 7,428 + ~12,000 (company-stated approximate for 2Q26), >5,000 returned to shareholders.

## Key Takeaways

- Capex peaked at 8.7% of sales (MXN 34,754; >US$2bn) in FY23 and fell to 5.3% (MXN 22,530; ~US$1.2bn) in FY25; 91% of FY25 capex went to manufacturing, logistics and sustainability [calc] and [sourced] RA25 p.81, p.220.
- M&A is a repeated, bolt-on-driven use of cash (MXN 5,988-11,294 per year in FY22-FY25) with committee and AGM thresholds at 3% and 20% of consolidated assets [sourced] RA25 p.196, p.220.
- Shareholder returns FY21-FY25 totalled MXN ~35.9bn (22.2bn dividends, 13.7bn buybacks); FY25 returns were 5,569, below FY22-FY24 [calc].
- Dividend per share rose to MXN 1.00 in 2025; the 2026 dividend is MXN 4,627 million but the per-share figure in the filing (1.75) conflicts with the share count (~1.08) [sourced] BMV-2Q26 p.97; [calc].
- Leverage went 1.5x (Dec-22) to 2.9x (Dec-24) to 2.5x (Jun-26); no numeric leverage or payout target is published in the filings reviewed [sourced].
