Of the 122 Bimbo press-release files, 43 are daily share counts, 20 are coupon notices, 11 are rating actions, 4 are offer notices, 6 are conference-call invitations or trading-volume statements, and about 38 are narrative corporate or ESG releases [calc from file names].
