## 1. Portfolio in one view

- ==More than 9,000 products under more than 100 brands==, in bakery (the core) and salty snacks [sourced] RA25 p.78, p.100. The annual report states bakery vs snacks is an organizational split, not separate lines of business [sourced] RA25 p.122-123.
- Core categories: packaged and artisan bread, buns/rolls, sweet bread, cakes and small cakes (pastelitos), cookies, toasted bread, English muffins, bagels, tortillas and flatbread, salty snacks, and others [sourced] RA25 p.78.
- Brand portfolio is built mostly by acquisition and then localised: global/regional umbrella brands plus strong local brands (see §3).
- Confectionery (Ricolino) was sold to Mondelez in November 2022 to focus on grain-based foods [sourced] RA25 p.76; details in [[bimbo-acquisitions]]. The portfolio today is "bakery and salty snacks".

## 4. Innovation

- **12.6% of FY25 net sales came from innovations in the portfolio** [sourced] RA25 p.80. Launches named: Artesano Masa Madre (sourdough) in Mexico and Latin America; Plusvita brioche in Brazil; new sizes/formats (Half Loaves in Mexico and the US, Open Top in Mexico); a simplified whole-grain bread in Mexico; the 100% Natural line in Iberia (no preservatives or added sugar); bagels with higher protein/fibre in the US and UK [sourced] RA25 p.80. In the US, "Thomas' High Protein Bagels" [sourced] RA25 p.88.
- Approach: consumer-occasion framework; innovation centres test prototypes and new ingredients; new products are approved by committees and market-tested [sourced] RA25 p.80, p.88.
- **Bimbo Ventures** (since 2016): invested in 60 startups and 12 venture funds through 2025; launched 10 open-innovation programmes in 2025; named 2025 collaborations: Oobli (sweet proteins), Dexterity (robotics), Soskende (grain/fruit/vegetable snacks), Rappi (e-commerce) [sourced] RA25 p.89. The company says it also feeds the M&A pipeline [sourced] RA25 p.89.
- **IP**: ~4,082 trademark files in Mexico and >20,947 abroad; 86 patents/utility models/industrial designs in Mexico and 82 abroad (granted and pending) [sourced] RA25 p.101.
- Brand registration gaps: "Bimbo" is registered by third parties in Chile, Puerto Rico and some European countries; "Marinela" in El Salvador and Honduras, where products are sold as Ideal and Marisela with Bimbo's own designs [sourced] RA25 p.101.

## 5. Nutrition and labelling programme (affects portfolio mix)

- The group aligns with the WHO global strategy on diet, physical activity and health since 2008, and uses internal nutrition guidelines in four axes: clean label, positive nutrition (Health Star Rating), fortification, smart portions [sourced] RA25 p.88.
- 2025 metrics: ~48% of net sales from products rated 3.5 stars or better (Health Star Rating); 100% of the daily-consumption portfolio free of artificial colours and 99% free of artificial flavours; 98% of the daily-consumption portfolio with "positive nutrition" [sourced] RA25 p.79, p.88; 4T25 p.2.
- Commitments: remove artificial colours from the entire portfolio by end-2026; all recipes "simple and natural" by 2030; 97% of white bread lines already at >=2 g fibre per 100 g with a target of 3 g [sourced] RA25 p.88.
- Responsible marketing: ICC code since 2022; no influencers under 13 in child-directed marketing; annual external audits [sourced] RA25 p.98-99.
- Related ESG articles: [[bimbo-esg-framework]], [[bimbo-sustainable-financing]].

## 7. Concentration and risk touchpoints

- No brand-level revenue concentration is disclosed. Customer concentration: Walmart ~15% of sales [sourced] RA25 p.103 ([[bimbo-business-model]]).
- Brand-name conflicts abroad (Bimbo in Chile/Puerto Rico; Marinela in El Salvador/Honduras) limit uniform branding [sourced] RA25 p.101.
- Product-labelling and nutrition rules in Mexico and other markets (front-of-pack, ingredient restrictions) are a regulatory theme; the legal framework is outlined in RA25 p.103-104 [sourced]; see [[bimbo-risk-factors]].

## Key Takeaways

- Portfolio is wide (9,000+ products, 100+ brands) but built around a handful of umbrella brands (Bimbo, Oroweat, Thomas', Marinela, Entenmann's, Barcel/Takis) plus strong local leaders [sourced] RA25 p.79, p.100-101.
- Leadership claims are strongest in Mexico (#1 packaged bread and cakes, 18.2% and 28.2% shares), US (largest bread supplier, 7.4% share, Thomas' in bagels/muffins) and Latin America (packaged-bread leader in each market) [sourced] RA25 p.83-84, p.110-111.
- Innovation contributes 12.6% of sales; the launches are mostly format, premium (sourdough, brioche, protein) and clean-label moves rather than new categories [sourced] RA25 p.80.
- Brand/category mix and private-label share are not disclosed; they must be sourced outside the filings.
- Nutrition programme (48% of sales at 3.5+ stars; artificial colours out by end-2026) is a portfolio-management cost and risk-mitigation theme; track in [[bimbo-esg-framework]].
