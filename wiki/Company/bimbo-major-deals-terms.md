## 2. Sara Lee NAFB and Iberia (2010-2011)

- **What was bought:** NAFB = Sara Lee's North American fresh bakery division, a royalty-free **perpetual licence to the Sara Lee brand** for fresh bakery in select geographies, 41 bakeries, 4,700 routes, 13,200 employees [sourced] MP-SL10 p.2, p.7.
- **The price fell as the business shrank.** NAFB EBITDA was US$132m (FY2009), US$122m (FY2010), US$108m (LTM to 2-Oct-2010) at announcement and **US$78m (LTM 30-Jun-2011)** by the revised deck; EV was cut from US$959m to US$709m after DOJ undertakings. EV fell 26% and EBITDA 28% [calc], so the multiple on the revised numbers (9.1x) was *higher* than at signing (8.9x) [sourced] MP-SL10 p.2, p.7; MP-SL11 p.4-5.
- **DOJ remedy (21-Oct-2011):** post-closing divestiture of Sara Lee and Earthgrains retail sliced bread, buns and rolls in California and smaller sets in Harrisburg/Scranton, Kansas City, Oklahoma City and Omaha, worth ~US$155m of revenue [sourced] MP-SL11 p.4, p.11.
- **Combination economics (Jun-2011 LTM):** Bimbo Bakeries USA revenue US$3,701m and EBITDA US$406m (11.0%) + NAFB US$2,036m and US$78m (3.8%) + synergies US$150m → pro forma EBITDA US$634m, 11.1% [sourced] MP-SL11 p.10. Group pro forma (incl. Iberia): revenue US$12,332m; EBITDA US$1,308m (10.6%), or US$1,458m (11.8%) with synergies [sourced] MP-SL11 p.21.
- **Leverage:** total debt/EBITDA 2.6x pro forma at Nov-2010 and 2.5x pro forma at Jun-2011, against 2.2x in 2010 [sourced] MP-SL10 p.4; MP-SL11 p.26. Debt after the deal US$3,088m [sourced] MP-SL11 p.24.
- **Iberia:** 7 plants, 800+ routes, 1,900 associates; Bimbo Spain was founded in Barcelona in 1963 by Jaime Jorba and sold in 1978, so the deal "reunites the Bimbo brand globally" [sourced] MP-SL11 p.2, p.15-16.
- **Precedent:** Sara Lee (through Hillshire Brands) acquired Earthgrains in 2001 by tender offer at **US$40.25 per share in cash**; the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period expired 20-Jul-2001 and the offer was extended to 7-Aug-2001 [sourced] `Earthgrains_2001-07-22_SC-TO-T-A_Hillshire-Brands` p.1. Earthgrains brands are among those later transferred to Bimbo via NAFB. The folder holds only the tender notices and GAMCO 13D/13G holding statements (3.12% then 2.40% positions), not deal multiples.

## 3. Canada Bread (2013-2014) — the best-documented deal

**Process** [sourced] CIRC-CB p.29-31:
1. 29-Aug-2013: Maple Leaf Foods (90.0003% owner, 22,875,215 of 25,416,812 shares) tells the board it is reviewing options; 4-Oct board forms an independent Special Committee; 18-Oct CIBC engaged; 21-Oct public announcement.
2. 24-Oct-2013: Olivieri fresh pasta sold to Ebro Foods for ~CAD120m (closed 25-Nov); CAD 8.00 special dividend paid 6-Jan-2014.
3. Mid-Oct to Jan: auction run by RBC Capital Markets and Centerview; proposals due 24-Jan-2014; Maple Leaf focuses on Bimbo on 28-Jan but asks for a higher price; Bimbo raises to **CAD 72.00** on 3-Feb; CIBC delivers its fairness opinion on 11-Feb; agreement 12-Feb.
4. Approvals: HSR clearance 24-Mar; shareholders (two-thirds required) 3-Apr; court 8-Apr; Investment Canada Act 13-May; close 23-May-2014; delisting ~26-May [sourced] `CanadaBread_2014-03-26_PR_Periodo-2014-03-26`, `…04-04…`, `…04-09…`, `…05-14…`, `…05-23…`.

**Terms** [sourced] CIRC-CB p.3, p.20, p.29, p.54-55:
- CAD 72.00/share = CAD1,830m [calc: 25,416,812 x 72.00 = CAD1,830m]; **31% premium** to the pre-announcement close of CAD61.25 (18-Oct-2013) **including the CAD8.00 special dividend**, and 34% to the 20-day VWAP of CAD59.58 [sourced]. Total value per share to holders, counting the dividend, was therefore about CAD80 [calc].
- Not subject to a financing condition. **Termination payment CAD 50m** (~2.7% of the price [calc]) payable by Canada Bread in specified circumstances, with reimbursement by Maple Leaf in certain cases; outside date 11-Aug-2014. Maple Leaf signed a voting support agreement and a non-competition agreement and supplies transitional services at cost.
- Quarterly dividends of up to CAD 0.75 were permitted until close; CAD 0.437 stub dividend paid with the consideration.

**Company-stated valuation and funding** [sourced] MP-CB p.2, p.5, p.8-9:
- Revenue CAD1,439m (fresh CAD972m, frozen CAD394m, UK CAD133m); EBITDA CAD185m (12.9%) versus CAD158m (2011, 9.9%) and CAD165m (2012, 10.5%); 25 bakeries, 5,450 employees. The CAD185m is Bimbo's "assessment of earnings capacity", not reported EBITDA.
- Pro forma Bimbo: revenue US$15.2bn, EBITDA US$1.5bn (9.7% margin); pro forma debt **US$4,546m**, debt/EBITDA **2.9-3.1x**, average tenor 5 years, average cost 3.7%.

**Outcomes** [sourced]:
- **Competition-law fine:** Canada Bread paid **CAD 50m (US$38m)** on 21-Jun-2023 to settle the Competition Bureau's packaged-bread price-fixing case, relating to two price increases "more than a decade ago" under Maple Leaf control; Canada Bread remains a defendant in two class actions [sourced] PR `Bimbo_2023-06-20_PR_Canada-Bread-resuelve-la`.
- **Claim against the seller:** on 29-Nov-2024 Grupo Bimbo sued Maple Leaf Foods and former officers in the Ontario Superior Court for **more than CAD 2bn** for alleged misrepresentation in the 2014 sale (alternatively unjust enrichment) [sourced] PR `Bimbo_2024-11-28_PR_Grupo-Bimbo-presenta-demanda-contra`. The folder holds no court ruling or defence.

## 4. Panrico, East Balt / Bimbo QSR, Mankattan (2015-2018)

- **Panrico:** agreed 9-Jul-2015 for €190m, excluding packaged sliced bread; €15m (MXN 281m) deposited; closed 21-Jul-2016 after CNMC (Spain) and Portuguese competition approval and a Supreme Court ruling on the 2013 labour-redundancy plan; results consolidated from 3Q16; brands Donuts, Qé!, Bollycao, La Bella Easo, Donettes; 9 plants, 2,000+ staff [sourced] `Bimbo_2017-05-08_AR_Informe-Anual-en` p.241, p.47; PR 2016-07-21. Sales ≈€280m per the transaction announcement [sourced] CNBV18 p.52.
- **East Balt / Bimbo QSR:** US$650m headline; US$514m cash after sellers' offsets; founded 1955 in Chicago, 21 plants and ~2,200 staff; purchase-price allocation: identifiable intangibles MXN 4,527m, goodwill **MXN 2,680m** after a MXN 415m reduction; 77-day 2017 contribution: sales MXN 2,175m, operating profit MXN 61m, net loss MXN 22m [sourced] CNBV19 p.125-126. Management: "approximately 3% of sales and 5% of EBITDA", funded by revolver and BIMBO 17 [sourced] PR 2017-10-15. QSR was later the platform for Food Town (Kazakhstan, 2020) [per [[bimbo-history-2014-2020]]].
- **Mankattan:** USD 200m = MXN 3,985m less MXN 368m brands, MXN 466m liabilities assumed and MXN 230m deposits = MXN 2,921m paid; producer of packaged bread, pastries, buns and yudane serving Beijing, Shanghai, Sichuan, Guangdong; seller Artal Group / Invus; Bimbo described itself as present in one Chinese market beforehand [sourced] CNBV19 p.123; PR 2018-02-11.
- **BBU voluntary separation programme (Jun-2018):** ~600 positions removed, **~US$100m non-cash charge in 2Q18**, payback under two years [sourced] PR `Bimbo_2018-06-25_PR_Bimbo-Bakeries-USA-Completa`.

## 6. Ricolino sale to Mondelez (2022)

- Business sold: Productos Ricolino, Productos de Leche Coronado, Ricolino S.A. de C.V. and the global brand portfolio (Ricolino, Vero, La Corona, Coronado); 2021 net sales MXN 10,147m (US$500m), ~6,000 employees, 4 plants, exports to 17 countries [sourced] PR 2022-04-24 p.1.
- EV MXN 27,000m is "adjusted at closing for cash, debt and other items"; **0.8% carve-out** (Nicaragua and Honduras antitrust approvals pending) so 99.2% of the proceeds were received at closing [sourced] PR 2022-10-31 p.1. The annual reports record MXN 25,797m collected (see [[bimbo-acquisitions]]); the PR EV and the collected amount differ by ~4.5% [calc] and the bridge is not in the folder.
- Rationale: focus on grain-based foods (bakery and snacks). Capital return: proposal for an extraordinary dividend of MXN 0.65/share, approved at the 17-Nov-2022 AGM [sourced] PR 2022-10-31; see [[bimbo-agm-dividends-and-buybacks]].

**Contradictions and extensions of existing claims**

- [[bimbo-history-2014-2020]] states that no EBITDA multiples are disclosed for 2014-2020 deals. Bimbo's own Canada Bread presentation prints **9.3x EV/EBITDA and 1.2x EV/revenue** and efficiencies of CAD40-65m by 2018 (the wiki's 9.9x is an equity-price calc on a different base).
- [[bimbo-history-2008-2013]] computes Sara Lee at 6.9x at closing on EBITDA of US$108m. The company's own Oct-2011 deck uses **EBITDA of US$78m (LTM Jun-2011)**, giving **9.1x** on EV US$709m; US$108m was the LTM to Oct-2010.
- Sara Lee synergy target moved from US$150-200m by 2013 (Nov-2010) to US$150m by 2014 (Oct-2011), with ~US$120m of integration cost and a US$1bn five-year investment commitment, none of which appear in the existing deal table.
- Canada Bread: the CAD72 price excludes a CAD8 special dividend paid before closing; the "31% premium" quoted in the release includes it.

## Key Takeaways

- ==The two best-documented deals were bought at 9.1x (Sara Lee NAFB, on collapsing EBITDA) and 9.3x (Canada Bread, on Bimbo's own "earnings capacity" EBITDA)== [sourced]; Iberia at 6.7x, Panrico about 0.7x sales and East Balt about 1.6x sales [calc].
- Sara Lee's EBITDA fell 28% between signing and the revised deck while EV fell 26%; synergies were re-timed from 2013 to 2014, an example of delayed deal accretion [calc].
