## Key Takeaways

- ==Bimbo spent 47.6bn on acquisitions in FY14–FY20; two deals (Canada Bread, 22.4bn of cash in FY14, and Bimbo QSR, most of the 12.5bn in FY17) account for about 73% of it and are the only ones that moved leverage (+1.1x and +0.7x).==
- Prices are disclosed (CAD 1,830m, EUR 190m/214m, US$650m, US$200m) but never EV multiples; price-to-sales ranged from about 0.7x (Panrico) to 1.8x (Mankattan) `[calc]`.
- Financing has been conservative and long: revolver first, then 10–30 year notes and peso certificates, plus one US$500m perpetual (equity-classified) in 2018; average maturity rose from 8.9 to 13.2 years.
