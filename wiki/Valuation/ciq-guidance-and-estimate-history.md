## Key Takeaways

- Management has missed or only just met its last EBITDA guide in FY2022-FY2024 and first guided too high three years running (FY2023 -4.8%, FY2024 -2.4%, FY2025 -10.0% vs first midpoint or floor); FY2026 started with a flat floor and has been raised to 60,465-64,470.
- Final consensus gets revenue right (MAE 1.1% over 26 years) and EBITDA close (2.9%) but EPS badly (12.8%; median -18.7% in 2011-2020): the forecast risk sits below EBITDA.
- EBITDA has beaten quarterly consensus in 66% of 79 quarters since 2004 (median +3.4%) while EPS normalized has beaten only 36 of 81 times, so the market is paying for margin execution that does not always reach EPS.
- Consensus target prices follow the share price (upside 0%-9% in 2009-2015; 29.5% now): the mean target is 70.17 vs a 95.14 peak at end-2023.
- FY2026 EPS consensus has halved since Sep-2023 (5.95 to 3.07) with EBITDA down 14.7%, and expected FY2026 net debt went from net cash to 154,430.
- Coverage is 8-11 analysts versus 14-15 in 2014-2017; estimates beyond FY2028 rest on 2.
