> The `Prospectus` stems are mislabelled. `…2023-05-31…-BIMBO-23` is the placement notice of **BIMBO 23-2L** and `…2023-05-31…-BIMBO-23L` is that of **BIMBO 23L**; for 30-May-2023 the two offer notices are swapped the other way. - Short-term CB programme of up to **MXN 5,000m** (maturities up to 365 days; MXN or US$), rated mxA-1+ by S&P (6-Dec-2024) and F1+(mex) by Fitch [sourced] PR `Bimbo_2024-12-06_PR_S-P-Global-Ratings-asigno` p.1; PR `Bimbo_2024-12-06_PR_Fitch-Asigna-Calificacion-F1-mex` p.1. - Common terms of every MXN issue: unsecured (*quirografario*), face value MXN 100, issue price par, single bullet at maturity, **guaranteed by Bimbo, S.A. de C.V., Barcel, S.A. de C.V. and Bimbo Bakeries USA, Inc.**, common representative Monex Casa de Bolsa, Indeval custody [sourced] AC/OA pages 1-3 of each notice. Default interest = coupon + 2 percentage points [sourced] OA BIMBO 17 p.2; AC 23L p.4.
- **Greenshoe always used with the Mexican "vasos comunicantes" (communicating vessels) structure** — the base book and over-allotment are shared across the fixed and floating series. 2023 and 2025: primary book MXN 10,000m + over-allotment MXN 5,000m = MXN 15,000m, fully taken [calc] from AC 23L p.2 (8,000+4,000), AC 23-2L p.1 (2,000+1,000), AC 25 p.2 (8,508+4,254), AC 25-2 p.2 (1,492+746). 2026: MXN 12,000m placed against a planned 10,000+5,000 [sourced] PR `Bimbo_2026-01-05_PR_S-P-National-Ratings-asigno` p.1.

- The existing wiki records the 23-2L 12.5% target as **not achieved** (see [[bimbo-sustainable-financing]]). ## 8. Covenants, events of default and change of control

The offering notices describe covenants only by reference to the supplement (*Suplemento*), which is **not in the folder**. What can be sourced:

- **Change of control:** if a Change of Control Event occurs, the issuer must offer to repurchase all certificates at **100% of nominal plus accrued interest**; the event is defined as a change of control that lowers the rating of the certificates [sourced] OA 17 p.2; AC 23L p.5; `Bimbo_2024-05-01_AR_Reporte-Anual-BMV-2023` p.16.
- **Summary of covenants across bank, international and local debt** (indicative) [sourced] CNBV20 p.110-111: affirmative — periodic financial information, legal existence and permits, use of proceeds, RNV registration for local notes, tax and labour compliance, pari passu ranking; negative — no change of core business, no merger/liquidation/sale of material assets, related-party deals only at arm's length, no restrictions on key subsidiaries' ability to pay dividends, no liens except permitted ones, and **in some credit facilities, interest-coverage and leverage ratios within set levels**; events of default — non-payment, false information, covenant breach, **cross-default or judgment above US$150m**, insolvency of the company or a material subsidiary, social-security non-payment, expropriation, repudiation of the local bonds, change of control.
- **Which financial covenants still apply.** At 31-Dec-2020 the company reported compliance with "leverage and interest coverage" ratios; the FY2022 integrated report reports compliance only with the **interest-coverage ratio** at 31-Dec-2022 and 2021, using "Conformed EBITDA" [sourced] CNBV20 p.109; `Bimbo_2023-06-03_AR_Prospecto-Definitivo` p.306 (this file is the FY2022 integrated annual report, mislabelled). The leverage covenant therefore appears to have been dropped from the facilities after 2020 [calc/inference].
- The threshold for the interest-coverage ratio is not disclosed anywhere in the folder [unverified].

## Key Takeaways

- Every MXN issue since 2017 is unsecured, guaranteed by Bimbo S.A., Barcel and BBU, placed through a shared fixed/floating "communicating vessels" structure; the programme ceiling rose from MXN 20bn (2023) to 35bn (2025) to 50bn (2026) [sourced] OA/AC.
- ==Fixed MXN spreads over the M Bono curve are tight and stable (+30 to +45 bp) with local AAA at both agencies since 2023, and the floating series paid 6.8-7.5% in 2026 versus 9.9-10.4% in March 2025 [sourced/calc].==
- BIMBO 23L carries a 25 bp coupon step-up from Nov-2031 (about MXN 30m a year) if the Scope 3 cut of 28% by 2030 is not verified; BIMBO 23-2L's 12.5% target carried a 0.20% redemption premium (MXN 6m) [sourced/calc].
- Dollar debt is issued at the BBU level (2029, 2034, 2036, 2051) and parent level (2044, 2047, 2049), all cross-guaranteed; the US$500m 5.95% perpetual was redeemed in Apr-2023 [sourced].
- S&P put BBB+ on negative outlook in Mar-2025 (net debt/EBITDA above 3x in 2025) and Fitch expects ~3.0x gross leverage at end-2025 easing to ~2.6x — the buyback pace in [[bimbo-agm-dividends-and-buybacks]] is a lever against that outlook [sourced].
- The only disclosed financial covenant after 2020 is interest coverage; its threshold, and the supplement-level covenants of each local issue, are not in the folder [unverified].
