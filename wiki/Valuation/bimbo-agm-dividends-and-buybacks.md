## 6. Bylaw provisions that bear on payouts and control

From the English bylaws on file (AML/KYC submission, 16-Jul-2024) [sourced] `Bimbo_2024-07-16_AML-KYC_Bylaws` p.15-16:

- **Article 17:** any transfer that takes a person or related group to 3% or more of the voting shares needs prior **board authorisation**; the board has three months to decide and, if it refuses, must appoint a buyer at the BMV price within 30 days; non-compliant transfers are not registered.
- **Article 17 Bis (tender offer):** anyone seeking **30% or more** of the ordinary shares must do so through a public offer for the greater of the proportional percentage or 10% of capital (if control is not sought), or 100% when seeking control; the board, after hearing the Audit Committee, must publish its opinion on price and conflicts within three months and may add an independent expert's opinion.
- The 2020 and 2023 bylaw versions in the same folder are image scans and unreadable (gap).
- Overall: a controlling block of ~72% ([[bimbo-governance-and-ownership]]) faces no practical takeover test, and buybacks that shrink the free float raise that block's percentage without any block sale.

## Key Takeaways

- ==Ordinary DPS rose from 0.29 (2017) to 1.075 (2026), but growth has slowed to ~1.5% a year since the 2021 reset to 1.00==, after an extraordinary 0.65 (Nov-2022, Ricolino proceeds) [sourced/calc].
- Buybacks are the swing factor: MXN 8.0bn in 2023-2024 (106m shares), then MXN 1.25bn in 2025 (22.7m shares) as S&P moved the outlook to negative; 2026 YTD pace is about 17m shares annualised [calc].
- Implied average buyback prices were 42 (2021), 62 (2022), 86 (2023), 69 (2024) and 55 (2025) pesos per share [calc]; against the 2025 year-end close of 59.12 [sourced via [[bimbo-shareholder-returns]]], the 2022-2024 purchases (MXN 10.6bn) were made above that level and only the 2021 and 2025 purchases below it.
- Share count is down 4.8% since 2020 with no issuance; all repurchased shares are cancelled each April, so the controlling block's percentage rises mechanically [calc].
- The 2026 dividend is MXN 1.075 per share (about MXN 4.63bn), confirmed against the AGM notice; the "1.75" in a regulatory filing is a typo [sourced].
- Gaps: April-2022 AGM notice, AGM minutes (results of votes), buyback ceilings voted each year, and 2017-2018 notices (illegible) are not in the folder.
