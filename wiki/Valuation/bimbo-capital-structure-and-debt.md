## 7. Covenants and key terms

- Change of control: if a change of control causes a decline in the rating of the Mexican bonds, Grupo Bimbo must offer to repurchase all of them at 100% of nominal plus accrued interest (RA25 p.6–7).
- Negative covenants: no change of main line of business; no merger / liquidation or sale of "important assets"; no restrictions on "important subsidiaries" paying dividends and capital to creditors; no liens except "permitted liens"; some credit agreements limit financial ratios; pari passu ranking (RA25 p.128).
- Affirmative covenants: periodic financial information, legal existence and permits, use of proceeds, RNV listing (for Mexican bonds), tax and labour compliance (RA25 p.128).
- Events of default: non-payment, false information, breach of covenants, cross-default / cross-acceleration above USD 150m or USD 200m depending on the instrument, bankruptcy, judgments above USD 150m (RA25 p.128).
- **Financial covenant:** interest coverage ratio (Conformed EBITDA / interest). Complied at 31-Dec-2025, 2024, 2023 and at 30-Jun-2026 (EEFF25 p.52; BMV Jun-26 p.56). **The required threshold is not disclosed** [unverified].
- Guarantees: all Mexican bonds, international notes of the parent, the revolver, BBVA and the bilateral credits are guaranteed by Bimbo SA de CV, Barcel SA de CV and Bimbo Bakeries USA; BBU notes are guaranteed by Grupo Bimbo, Bimbo and Barcel; subsidiary bilaterals are guaranteed by the parent (EEFF25 p.52).
- Debt capacity risk factors (additional debt may limit dividends, flexibility) are in RA25 p.42.

## Key Takeaways

- Total debt is stable at about MXN 153.7bn (Dec-25 and Jun-26) while net debt fell from 145.1bn to 137.1bn and net debt / Adj. EBITDA from 2.7x to 2.5x, all on an ex-IFRS 16 basis; leases add a further 33-35bn outside net debt.
- The stack at Dec-25 is 53% USD senior notes (4,543 USD m, 2029–2051), 31% Mexican bonds and about 16% bank and other loans [calc: 81,619 / 47,339 / residual 24,705 of 153,663, net of issuance costs].
- Average maturity has shortened from 16.4 years (Dec-21) to 9.5 years (Jun-26) and the average cost sits at 6.5%; the near-term wall is small (2026: about USD 253m, 2027: about USD 658m, 2028: about USD 833m per the 2Q26 chart).
- Liquidity is anchored on an undrawn USD 2,350m revolver (A 2030 / B 2028) plus cash of MXN 16.6bn at Jun-26; the only financial covenant disclosed is interest coverage and the company reports compliance.
- Ratings are BBB+ / BBB+ / Baa1 (S&P / Fitch / Moody's) as stated in the filings; outlooks and the covenant threshold are not disclosed and must be sourced externally.
