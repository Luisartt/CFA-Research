## 3. Cost and tenor drift

- Weighted fixed coupon rose from 5.00% (FY21) to 6.56% (2Q26) [calc] as 3.875–4.88% USD notes were replaced by 6.05–6.40% USD and 9.2–10.1% MXN paper.

## Key Takeaways

- Principal ex leases grew from 84,741 (FY22) to 154,497 (2Q26) [calc], with MXN bonds rising from 17,339 to 51,639 and bank debt from 7,522 to 23,495.
- Refinancing was rolling and pre-emptive: new 2049, 2051, 2029/2034/2036 USD notes each preceded the retirement of a near-term USD bond; from 2025 issuance shifted to MXN CBs and EUR/CAD bank lines.
- The cost of fixed debt rose from 5.00% (FY21) to 6.56% (2Q26) and weighted tenor fell from 15.8 to 9.4 years [calc]; the floating share rose from ~0% to 19% [calc].
- Long USD notes were partly bought back in 2022 (2044, 2049, 2051; about USD 52m in total [calc: 2.5 + 34.9 + 14.7 from implied balances]).
- CIQ reproduces company total and net debt exactly for Dec-20 and Dec-23 to Jun-26 after removing leases and derivatives; FY21–FY22 differ by +67 and +590.
