## Key Takeaways

- Over 18 months the FY2026 consensus fell 8.5% on revenue, 6.4% on EBITDA and 21% on EPS; expected FY2026 net debt rose 25% (to 146,914). The last three months were nearly flat.
- Revision breadth (3 months): revenue and EPS revisions skew down (FY2026 EPS 1 up / 5 down), EBITDA and EBIT revisions skew up (FY2027 EBITDA 4 up / 1 down).
- Since 2024 Bimbo has usually met or beaten EBITDA consensus but missed EPS in 8 of 10 quarters — the gap sits below EBITDA (D&A, interest, tax, minorities).
- 2Q26 (23-Jul-2026) missed on revenue (−0.9%), EBITDA (−0.9%) and EPS (−9.3%) after a 1Q26 beat; the next test is 3Q26, expected 22-Oct-2026.
