# Grupo Bimbo — Quarterly Trend 1Q08–4Q13

Quarterly net sales, EBITDA (UAFIDA) and majority net income for 24 quarters, built from the company's quarterly earnings releases (the BMV quarterly filings carry the same figures; the 3Q12 BMV filing is used as a cross-check). Units: ==MXN millions==. Annual view in [[bimbo-income-statement-fy08-fy13]]; the next period is [[bimbo-quarterly-trend-4q21-2q26]] (FY2014–FY2020 not covered).

**Method.** Each fiscal year's four quarters are taken from the quarterly income statement table printed in the fourth-quarter release (the page headed "Estado de Resultados" with 1T–4T columns), because those tables are the restated series that add up to the audited annual totals. First-published quarter figures (the release for that quarter) are listed in section 2 so drift is visible. NIF through FY2011 (original basis), IFRS for FY2011 restated and FY2012–FY2013. Quarterly EBITDA is the company's UAFIDA, defined as operating profit after general expenses plus D&A under NIF and operating income plus D&A, impairment and MEPP under IFRS `[sourced]` RA12 p.149 footnote (`filings/bmv/Reporte_Anual_2012_FINAL.pdf`). Margins are `[calc]`.

> [!warning] Basis breaks
> (1) FY2008 sales and net income are shown restated (the four 2008 quarters in the 4Q08 release sum to the audited 82,317). (2) FY2009 uses the 4Q10 release restatement. (3) FY2011 appears under NIF (4Q11 release) and IFRS (4Q12 release); the IFRS quarterly series does not tie to the audited IFRS FY2011 (see Flags). (4) FY2013 4Q comes from the 4Q13 release, which is not consistent with the 3Q13 year-to-date (see Flags). Do not chain YoY across the NIF/IFRS break: FY2012 YoY is against IFRS-restated FY2011.

## 4. Reading the trend

- **Seasonality.** 4Q carries 26–27% of sales (31% in 2011, with Sara Lee and Iberia consolidated) but 26–33% of EBITDA `[calc]`: Q4 share of annual EBITDA was 32.8% (2008), 31.7% (2009), 25.9% (2010), 30.3% (2011 NIF), 28.8% (2012), 28.7% (2013). The 4Q-over-1Q margin gap was 4.7 pp (2008) and 6.0 pp (2009: 16.7% vs 10.7%), then only 0.6, 0.1, 1.9 and 2.9 pp in 2010–2013 (4Q13 10.7% vs 1Q13 7.8%) `[calc]`.
- **2009 step.** Sales ran at ~28–30bn per quarter from 1Q09 against 19–22bn in 2008, and EBITDA margin jumped from 9.8% (1Q08) to 16.7% (4Q09) as US EBITDA turned from 539 for the year to 5,727 `[sourced]` RA09 p.88.
- **2010–2011 plateau and erosion.** 2010 quarterly sales were flat (−0.1% to +1.6%) and 4Q10 EBITDA fell 20.2% against a very strong 4Q09: gross margin fell 1.4 pp to 51.9% on raw-material costs, selling and distribution spend rose, and the quarter carried non-cash extraordinary charges of 346 in Latin America (Brazil legal contingencies) and 222 in Mexico (acquisition-related holding expenses), which took 3.5 pp off the EBITDA margin `[sourced]` `ER_BIMBO_4T10_ESP_0.pdf` p.1; RA11 p.100. 2011 quarterly EBITDA fell year on year in 1Q–3Q (−9.9%, −12.5%, −2.0%) before 4Q11 grew 14.8% with Sara Lee included `[calc]`.
- **2012 trough.** Quarterly EBITDA margin was 7.1–9.0% in 2012 under IFRS against 10.4–13.0% in 2011 NIF and 9.4–12.2% in 2011 IFRS; net margin fell to 0.4% in 4Q12 on integration costs (1,950 for the year), MEPP and a 47.4% annual tax rate `[sourced]` RA12 p.99–100. 3Q12 operating income was only 1,342 vs 3,070 in 3Q11 as other expenses hit 1,662 `[sourced]` `PR_4T12_0.pdf` p.9.
- **2013 recovery.** EBITDA grew 11.5%, 27.8%, 29.4% and 22.7% year on year across the four quarters with sales up only 0.3–3.8%; the margin returned to 10.7–10.8% in 3Q13–4Q13, reflecting gross margin recovery (4Q13 52.9%, +160 bp) and US synergies `[sourced]` `PR_4T13_0.pdf` p.1. 4Q13 EBITDA margin of 10.7% was +170 bp year on year `[sourced]`.

## 5. Flags

- IFRS FY2011 quarterly series (4Q12 release p.9) sums to EBITDA 14,309 and majority net income 5,091, against audited IFRS 14,719 and 4,875 (RA12 p.11–13); the 4Q12 release itself prints FY2011 EBITDA 14,310 and net income 5,090 `[sourced]` `PR_4T12_0.pdf` p.6–7. Use the quarterly series for shape, the audited figures for levels `[unverified]` reason.
- FY2013: the quarters add to sales 176,761 and net income 4,270; the 4Q13 release prints 12M sales 176,040 (implying a lower 9M of 129,558 than the 130,282 printed in the 3Q13 release) and net income 4,384 `[sourced]` `PR_4T13_0.pdf` p.2, p.5; `PR_3T13_0.pdf` p.8. Audited FY2013 minus the 3Q13 nine-month figures implies 4Q13 sales 45,759, EBITDA 4,936 and net income 1,410 `[calc]`. The 4Q13 release is also preliminary against the audited annual report (operating income 10,469 vs 10,490).
- Q4 2012 as first published (45,276 sales, 4,082 EBITDA) differs from the 3Q13 release's restated 4Q12 (45,278; 4,058); EBITDA FY2012 as published in the 4Q12 release is 14,100 vs audited 14,076.
- The 4Q09 release prints FY2009 sales of 116,479; the audited value is 116,353 (a 126 reclassification).
- Net-income quarterly figures are majority interest after non-controlling interests; EPS by quarter is not extracted.
- 2008 sales YoY uses 2007 quarters in constant pesos, so 2008 YoY is not exactly like for like.

## Key Takeaways

- ==Quarterly sales stepped from ~20bn (2008) to ~28–30bn (2009) on Weston Foods and to ~41–46bn (from 4Q11/1Q12) on Sara Lee and Iberia==; organic sales growth in 2010 and 2013 was 0.7% and 1.7% `[sourced]`.
- Quarterly EBITDA margin peaked at 16.7% in 4Q09 and bottomed at 7.1% in 1Q12; it was back to 10.7% by 4Q13 `[sourced]`/`[calc]`.
- Quarterly net margin fell to 0.4% in 4Q12 and recovered to 2.7% in 4Q13 `[calc]`.
- 4Q is the seasonal peak: 26–33% of annual EBITDA (29–33% in five of six years) from 26–27% of sales `[calc]`.
- Reported 2009 growth rates of 41–47% were 10–20% on a pro-forma basis, so use the pro-forma tables when judging organic momentum `[sourced]`.
- Figures drift by up to ~1% between first publication, restated comparatives and audited totals; always cite the filing that reconciles to the audited annual number.
