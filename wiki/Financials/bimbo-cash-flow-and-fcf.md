## 3. Free cash flow

### 3a. As the company defines it
- **FY2021** definition (4Q21 release): free cash flow for the 12 months ==before acquisitions, dividends and share buybacks== `[sourced]` 4Q21 release p.2 footnote 5. FY2021 amount: ==MXN 10,984== `[sourced]` 4Q21 release p.2.
- **2026** definition (1Q26/2Q26 releases): Free Cash Flow = Adjusted EBITDA **without IFRS 16**, minus capex (acquisitions not included), minus taxes and net interest paid, plus working-capital change of the period `[sourced]` 2Q26 release p.2 footnote 3.
- **Amounts disclosed**: 1Q26 ==7,428== (+4,633 vs 1Q25, implying 1Q25 = 2,795 `[calc]`) `[sourced]` 1Q26 release p.1; 2Q26: around 12,000 `[sourced]` 2Q26 release p.1–2. No FY2022–FY2025 company-defined FCF figure appears in the documents reviewed.

> [!question] Period of the "around MXN 12,000m" FCF
> The 2Q26 release states the figure in the quarter highlights and in the CFO quote without saying whether it is the quarter or the first half. Reasoning `[calc]`: (1) 1Q26 alone was 7,428, so a 12,000 first-half total implies about 4,572 for 2Q26; (2) net debt fell by only 654 in 2Q26 (137,751 → 137,097) after about 5,200 of dividends and buybacks, which is not compatible with 12,000 in the quarter; (3) net debt fell by 8,031 in 1H26 (145,128 → 137,097), consistent with ~12,000 of first-half FCF minus ~5,300 returned and ~235 of acquisitions, plus peso appreciation on USD/EUR debt. The first-half reading fits, but it is not stated `[unverified]`. Confirm on the 2Q26 earnings call before quoting.

## Discrepancies and open points

- CFO FY21 is 45,776 in RA21, RA22 and RA23 (no restatement) while restated EBITDA is 47,372 (continuing only); the cash-flow statement includes Ricolino `[sourced]` RA21 selected financial data; RA23 EEFF p.11.
- FY24 "Gain on disposal of discontinued operation": the statement shows 277 as an add-back and (277) for the discontinued loss before tax, so they offset `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.16.
- Company "returned to shareholders" (e.g. 8,666 in FY24) vs dividends declared plus buybacks (8,558) vs cash-flow lines (8,667): three definitions `[calc]`.
- Free cash flow: no FY22–FY25 company-defined figures; only FY21 (10,984), 1Q26 (7,428) and 2Q26 (~12,000, period unclear).

## Key Takeaways

- ==CFO recovered to a record 47,753 in FY25 (+20%)== as capex fell from 34,754 (FY23 peak) to 22,530 and working capital stopped draining cash `[sourced]`.
- CFO is struck before interest and lease payments, which together took 19,593 (41%) of FY25 CFO; a levered FCF proxy was 6,216 in FY25 versus 24,912 before leases and interest `[calc]`.
- FY23–FY24 were funded by debt: net borrowing of 26.8bn and 22.6bn against negative proxy FCF `[calc]`; 1H26 reverses the pattern (proxy 8,986; capex only 3.4% of sales).
- Capital returns are rising again: dividend MXN 1.75 per share for 2026 (4,626.7m) versus 1.00; 1H26 returns >5,000 (dividends 4,785 + buybacks 496) `[sourced]`.
- Company-defined FCF is disclosed only sporadically (FY21 10,984; 1Q26 7,428; ~12,000 in the 2Q26 release with an unclear period); the model should build FCF from CFO and the lines above rather than rely on it.
