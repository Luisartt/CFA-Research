## Key Takeaways

- ==Ex-FX sales growth has been +2% to +5% for five straight quarters (+2.0% in 3Q25, +3.9%, +4.8%, +4.5% in 2Q26)==, up from about zero in 2024–1H25, but MXN reported growth is negative in 4Q25–2Q26 because of peso strength `[sourced]`.
- The group Adj. EBITDA margin has moved from 12.4% (1Q25) to 14.4% (2Q26); the 14.0% in 1Q26 includes a one-off gain on an associate sale (amount undisclosed) `[sourced]`.
- North America (about 43% of sales) went from a 5.9% EBITDA margin (4Q24) to 10.0% (2Q26) and returned to positive ex-FX growth in 1Q26 `[sourced]`; Mexico holds 20–22%; EAA reached 13.8% in 4Q25; Latin America slipped to 5.1% (4Q25) and 7.7% (2Q26) on Wickbold integration `[sourced]`.
- Net income majority fell year-on-year in each of the 11 quarters from 1Q23 to 3Q25 and is growing again since 4Q25 (+1.2%, +32.3%, +3.9%) `[sourced]`; 1Q26's +32% is flattered by the associate gain.
- Quarterly figures are revised after the fact (sales by up to ~1.1bn per year); use restated comparatives, and note that first-reported quarters do not sum to the audited year in 2023–2025 `[calc]`.
