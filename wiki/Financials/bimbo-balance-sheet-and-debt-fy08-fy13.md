# Grupo Bimbo — Balance Sheet, Debt and Leverage FY2008–FY2013

How the 2009 Weston Foods deal and the 2011 Sara Lee, Iberia and Fargo deals reshaped the balance sheet: debt from MXN 11bn to 46bn, goodwill and intangibles from 19% to ~42% of assets, pension liabilities from ~1bn to ~20bn. Units: ==MXN millions== unless stated; year-end balances. Companion to [[bimbo-income-statement-fy08-fy13]]; later period in [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-leverage]] (FY2021+).

**Citation key.** RA08–RA13 = annual reports to the BMV for FY2008–FY2013 (`filings/bmv/BIMBO_Reporte_Anual_2008_esp_2.pdf`, `Grupo_Bimbo_Reporte_Anual_CNBV_2009_25.06.10_ESP.pdf`, `Bimbo_ReporteAnual_2010_port_1_0.pdf`, `infoanua_0.pdf` (FY2011), `Reporte_Anual_2012_FINAL.pdf`, `Grupo_Bimbo_Reporte_Anual_2013__0.pdf`). `p.` = PDF page index. Debt = short-term debt and current portion + long-term debt, as in the balance sheet; net debt = debt − cash `[calc]`.

> [!warning] GAAP break and reclassifications
> FY2008–FY2011 are NIF; FY2011 restated and FY2012–FY2013 are IFRS (transition 1-Jan-2011) `[sourced]` RA12 p.209. The FY2010 column also exists in IFRS (opening balance sheet, RA12 p.12). RA10 regrossed the FY2008 and FY2009 balance sheets (FY2009 total assets 96,713 in RA09 vs 99,666 in RA10; FY2008 58,506 vs 60,210), by adding the same amount to receivables and to payables `[sourced]` RA09 p.13; RA10 p.14. Do not compare totals across RA09 and RA10.

- Management guided pro forma total debt / EBITDA of 2.9–3.1x after the Canada Bread acquisition announced 12-Feb-2014 `[guidance]` RA13 p.216.

## Key Takeaways

- ==Weston Foods (2009) took total debt / EBITDA from 1.13x to 2.32x and net debt from 3.8bn to 31.8bn==; Sara Lee, Iberia and Fargo (2011) took it to 3.00x (NIF) or 3.12x (IFRS) `[sourced]`.
- Deleveraging was real but slow: debt fell by 5.7bn from FY2011 to FY2013 and EBITDA recovered 17.7%, bringing total debt / EBITDA to 2.33x; Bimbo guided 2.9–3.1x pro forma for Canada Bread `[calc]`/`[guidance]`.
- Goodwill and intangibles are ~42% of FY2013 total assets after 2009 and 2011 (18.7% in FY2008); a margin shortfall at BBU is therefore also an impairment risk `[calc]`.
- Pension and multi-employer plan liabilities went from 982 to ~20bn between FY2008 and FY2012 and are not in the company's leverage ratios; including them FY2012 adjusted leverage is materially higher `[calc]`.
- Debt was refinanced from bank/bridge to bonds in 2009–2012: US$800m notes (4.875% 2020, 4.50% 2022), MXN CEBURES (2009, 2012) and committed revolvers; average life went from 3.2 years (2009) to 5.9 years (2012) `[sourced]`.
- Cash was thin: FY2013 closed with 2.5bn cash against 8.0bn of current debt maturities and a undrawn US$2bn revolver `[sourced]`.
