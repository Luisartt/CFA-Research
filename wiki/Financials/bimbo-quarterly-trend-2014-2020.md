> [!warning] Read before using any quarter
> 1. **Every figure below is "as first reported"** in the release for that quarter (for 1Q14–3Q14 the numbers are taken from the four-quarter table printed in the 4T14 release, p.8). Later releases restated prior-year comparatives (log in section 6). The four first-reported quarters therefore do not sum to the year-end figure in 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019 and 2020 (table in section 5).
> 2. **Regions changed in 2017.** Europe (to 1Q17) became EAA (from 2Q17) when China moved out of Mexico; the 1Q17 first release still shows the old mapping. The 2Q16–4Q16 EAA values in later releases are 230–284 higher per quarter and Mexico lower by the same amount.
> 3. **EBITDA basis breaks twice.** 1Q14–4Q19 headline Adj. EBITDA is **pre-IFRS 16** (rent expensed; IFRS 16 starts 1Q19, and the 2019 headline stays pre-IFRS 16); from 1Q20 the headline is **IFRS 16 basis**. The 2019 quarters on the new basis are in table 5. A YoY EBITDA growth rate across 4Q19→1Q20 on the first-reported numbers is meaningless; the company's own 2020 growth rates compare IFRS 16 to IFRS 16 `[sourced]` 1T20 p.5; 4T20 p.6.
> 4. **Adjusted vs reported.** Adj. EBITDA excludes impairments, MEPP and (2Q18, 3Q18) VSP/restructuring charges; net income is *reported* (2Q18 195 vs 1,880 adjusted; 1Q20 20 vs 1,880 adjusted for the MEPP charge) `[sourced]` 2T18 p.2; 1T20 p.6.

## Key Takeaways

- ==28 quarters of first-reported data show a steady margin expansion (Adj. EBITDA margin 7.1%–12.7% in FY14–FY19 pre-IFRS 16; 12.0%–15.1% in 2020 on IFRS 16) with Q1 the seasonal low (9.1% average) and Q4 the high.==
- Quarterly net income is volatile because of non-cash items: 4Q14 (−231, MEPP), 4Q16 (86, Latin America impairments, 87% tax rate), 4Q17 (427, 75% tax rate), 2Q18 (195, VSP), 1Q20 (20, MEPP); use adjusted EBITDA for trend.
- First-reported quarters do not sum to the year and regional comparatives were rewritten in 2016, 2017 and 2019; always cross-check against the next-year comparative and the log in section 6.
- 2Q20 (+19.9%) is the strongest quarter in the series that was not acquisition-driven: North America +36.4% in pesos (+11.5% in dollars) on volume, with Mexico between −1.2% and +1.6% after +6.2% in 1Q20. Earlier growth above 12% (1Q15 +19.9%, 3Q14 +11.9%, FY16 +15.0%) came from acquisitions and FX.
- Net debt behaviour is visible in quarterly debt: 37.9bn (1Q14) to 60.6bn (2Q14) on Canada Bread; 120.1bn at 1Q20 (revolver draw) back to 85.2bn at 4Q20.
