# Grupo Bimbo — Income Statement FY1998–FY2007 (Mexican GAAP, constant pesos)

Ten fiscal years of the consolidated P&L, regional sales, operating income and EBITDA before the IFRS era covered in [[bimbo-income-statement-fy21-fy25]]. Units: ==MXN millions== unless stated, **in the constant pesos of each report's own year-end** (inflation-restated, Mexican GAAP / NIF). Fiscal year = calendar year. Companion articles: [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-debt-fy98-fy07]] (balance sheet, debt, capex) and [[bimbo-history-1998-2007]] (events, M&A, management). Related: [[bimbo-acquisitions]], [[bimbo-capital-allocation]], [[bimbo-segments-and-regions]].

**Citation key.** `p.` = PDF page index of the file (the printed page number is usually 2–5 lower). AR98 … AR07 = `filings/annual/INFORME ANUAL 1998.pdf` … `INFORME ANUAL 2007.pdf` (AR01 = `INFORME ANUAL 2001_0.pdf`; `_1` is a byte-identical copy). AR07 is word-for-word the same document as `filings/bmv/GrupoBimbo_BVM_RA_20080923_esp_0.pdf` (the FY2007 Reporte Anual to the BMV). BMV02 = `filings/bmv/GrupoBimbo_BVM_RA_20020624_esp_0.pdf` (FY2001 annual report filed 24-Jun-2002); BMV03/BMV04 = the 26-Jun-2003 and 29-Jun-2004 filings (FY2002, FY2003). "UAFIDA" is the company's EBITDA (operating income plus depreciation and amortisation).

> [!warning] Comparability: never splice these years as one series
> Every year is stated in the pesos of its own December and is re-stated again, differently, in the next report (section 3). Seven accounting or presentation breaks fall inside the window:
> 1. **1998, Bulletin B-15:** foreign subsidiaries (Mrs. Baird's, Latin America) are indexed by local inflation and translated at the closing FX rate, instead of Mexican INPC; 1997 and earlier are not comparable [sourced] AR98 p.7, p.16.
> 2. **2000, Bulletin D-4:** deferred income tax is recognised for the first time; a MXN 1,747m cumulative charge goes straight to equity and a MXN 1,455m deferred-tax liability appears [sourced] AR00 p.24, p.27.
> 3. **2002, EITF 01-09 (US GAAP) and IAS 39 applied by supplementary reference:** promotional ("merchandising") costs are netted against sales, and commodity-derivative results move from other income into cost of sales; the company itself says margins are affected [sourced] AR02 p.24-25.
> 4. **2002, Weston consolidation (4-Mar-2002):** US sales +73.3% (−4.0% without Weston); FY2002 group growth of 18.3% is mostly inorganic [sourced] AR02 p.6, p.25.
> 5. **2003, Bulletin C-15 (impairment):** a MXN 1,864m (text) / 1,834m (income statement line) charge net of tax; and a MXN 1,606m extraordinary tax recovery [sourced] AR03 p.32-35, p.41.
> 6. **2005, Bulletin B-7:** goodwill stops being amortised, so "other expenses" fall by ~MXN 400m with no change in cash [sourced] AR05 p.34.
> 7. **2007, NIF B-3:** employee profit sharing (PTU) and what used to be "special" items move below the operating line; the headline line becomes "utilidad después de gastos generales" (profit after general expenses), which is *before* other income/expenses and PTU. FY2006 PTU of MXN 456m was reclassified [sourced] AR07 p.10, p.12.
>
> From 1-Jan-2008 inflation accounting is suspended (NIF B-10, "non-inflationary environment" below 26% three-year inflation) [sourced] AR07 p.133, so FY2007 is the last year in inflation-restated pesos.

## 6. What drove each year (company explanations)

- **FY1998:** Mrs. Baird's consolidated from May (sales 2,237 and operating profit 77 in the stub); FX loss of 511 on dollar debt; Latin America lost 321 at operating level, Argentina and Peru start-up named [sourced] AR98 p.4, p.11, p.14.
- **FY1999:** record margins (gross 54.8%, operating 9.9%, net 6.8%) helped by the peso, lower wheat prices and a 133 gain on the mill sale; leverage fell from 33.9% to 13.6% net debt to equity after the 13-May-1999 share offering [sourced] AR99 p.4, p.17-18.
- **FY2000:** gross margin 56.2% ("highest in 15 years"), operating margin 10.3% ("highest in 6 years"), but net income −13.4% because monetary-position gain collapsed (MXN 4m vs 508m in 1999) and a US reorganisation loss hit; no price increases in core Mexican lines [sourced] AR00 p.3, p.6, p.19-20, p.25.
- **FY2001:** one-offs inside operating and other lines (ERP, route restructuring, new routes in Brazil, US provisions for asset sales and two plant closures; a recalculation of the employee salary subsidy); Plus Vita lost 76 at operating level in nine months [sourced] AR01 p.30-31, p.41.
- **FY2002:** Weston consolidated for ten months (US sales 10,810); non-recurring charges for Texas route sales, the Dallas plant, Vienna closure, Abastex (Mexico grocery retail distribution) and a US pension reserve; CIF more than doubled to 691 on the acquisition debt [sourced] AR02 p.24-27.
- **FY2003:** operating income +11.1% on cost relief in the second half; two offsetting items (tax recovery +1,606 and C-15 impairment −1,834); US operating margin −3.3% (−1.4% excluding charges of 186) [sourced] AR03 p.32-35.
- **FY2004:** operating margin 8.0%; CIF down 45% on lower debt; US and Latin America losses narrowed by 33.5% and 46.8%; 15% of US SKUs eliminated; Joyco consolidated from 13-May-2004 [sourced] AR04 p.23-26.
- **FY2005:** best results in company history (sales +6.7%, operating income +21.7%); wheat flour and sugar costs fell and the peso strengthened; goodwill amortisation ended (B-7); El Globo and La Corona added 1.4 points to Mexican sales growth [sourced] AR05 p.31-34.
- **FY2006:** wheat and corn averaged +38% and +27%; gross margin −0.5 points to 53.5% offset by lower administration cost; Latin America sales +21.8% [sourced] AR06 p.32-34.
- **FY2007:** sales +8.2%, Latin America +21.1% (77,000 new customers); gross margin 52.8% (−0.5 points); net income +3.5% because of non-cash deferred-tax adjustments (US asset value after 2006 US deflation, and the Mexican IETU from 1-Jan-2008); ex-deferred tax net income would have been 4,010, +8.9% [sourced] AR07 p.75-77.

## Key Takeaways

- Net sales grew from MXN 25.1bn (FY1998) to 72.3bn (FY2007) in each year's pesos; chain-linking the company's real growth gives ==~1.9x in real terms==, of which the 2002 Weston deal is ~21 index points.
