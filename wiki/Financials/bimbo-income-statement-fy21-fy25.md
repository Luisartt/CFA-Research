## Key Takeaways

- ==Net sales grew from 338.8bn (FY21 restated) to 427.0bn (FY25)==, but growth since FY22 is slow in MXN: +0.3%, +2.1%, +4.6%, and +0.5% / +1.6% ex-FX in FY24 and FY25 `[sourced]`.
- Adj. EBITDA is a steady 13.4–13.9% of sales; FY25 hit a record 59,456 (+7.2%) with a 13.9% margin, while operating income was flat at ~34bn because D&A rose from 18.9bn (FY23) to 24.8bn (FY25) `[sourced]`.
- Net income majority has fallen three years in a row (15.5bn, 12.5bn, 11.1bn) as net financing cost rose from 10.4bn to 14.0bn and the tax rate went back to 35.7% `[sourced]`.
- ==Mexico is the profit engine (20.4% EBITDA margin; 54% of segment EBITDA)==; North America's EBITDA margin dropped from 12.2% (FY21) to 8.4% (FY24) and recovered to 9.0% (FY25) `[calc]`; EAA doubled EBITDA from 2.7bn to 5.8bn `[sourced]`.
- FY21, FY22 and FY24 contain material one-offs (Ricolino, MEPP, restructuring); use the restated FY21 base and strip one-offs before comparing growth.
