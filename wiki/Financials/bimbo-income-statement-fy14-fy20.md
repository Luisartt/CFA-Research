> [!warning] Seven accounting and presentation breaks inside FY2014–FY2020 — never splice these series silently
> 1. **FY2013 base restated.** The 4T14 release shows FY13 sales of 174,623 and EBITDA of 17,251 `[sourced]` 4T14 p.8; the audited FY14 annex restates FY13 to 176,041 / 17,326 (Latin America 21,822 vs 20,405) `[sourced]` RA15 p.286. Cause not stated in the text reviewed `[unverified]`; FY14 growth is 6.3% on the restated base and 7.1% on the original `[sourced]` 4T14 p.1.
> 2. **Regional re-mapping in 2017.** Until 1Q17 the segments were Mexico (which included China, "debido a su poca importancia" RA15 p.141), North America, Latin America and Europe. From 2Q17 the company reports **EAA (Europe, Asia and Africa)**: China moved out of Mexico. Recast FY15 Mexico 75,597 vs 76,295 first reported (−698) and EAA 8,258 vs Europe 7,560 (+698); FY16 Mexico 81,455 vs 82,386 (−931) and EAA 12,607 vs Europe 11,676 (+931) `[calc]` from `[sourced]` RA18 p.373 and 4T15 p.11 / 4T16 p.10. FY14 and FY13 are not recast in any document reviewed.
> 3. **1-Jan-2018 adoptions: IFRS 9, IFRS 15, IFRIC 23, IAS 29 (Argentina).** Comparatives were not restated; the cumulative effect went straight to equity: IFRIC 23 −2,283; IFRS 9 +32; IFRS 15 −157; IAS 29 −2,180 `[sourced]` RA18 p.186. IAS 29 monetary-position effects enter the CIF from 2018 (gain 202 in FY18, loss 114 in FY19, gain 70 in FY20) `[sourced]` RA20 p.195.
> 4. **Reclassification of payments to customers (RA19).** FY17 net sales rise from 267,515 to 268,485 (+970) and FY18 from 288,266 to 289,320 (+1,054); gross profit +968 / +1,052; distribution and selling +1,064 / +1,071; operating income unchanged `[sourced]` RA19 p.232 (note 23, "cambio en presentación de pagos efectuados a clientes"). The same document restates FY18 EBITDA to 31,700 `[sourced]` 4T19 p.2 (vs 31,705).
> 5. **IFRS 16 from 1-Jan-2019, modified retrospective.** FY18 and earlier are not restated `[sourced]` RA19 p.209. FY19 operating expenses, D&A and interest are therefore not comparable with FY18: D&A jumps from 10,000 to 14,373 and administration falls to 16,641 `[sourced]` RA19 p.197-200.
> 6. **Adjusted EBITDA basis switch.** The 4T19 release headlines FY19 Adj. EBITDA at 33,426 (rent expensed, comparable with FY18) and shows 37,873 as "UAFIDAR Ajustada" (rent added back) `[sourced]` 4T19 p.2. From 1Q20 the headline is the IFRS 16 figure and FY19 is shown as 37,873 `[sourced]` 4T20 p.2. FY18 31,705 and FY19 37,874 sit side by side in the RA20 table without recast `[sourced]` RA20 PDF p.15.
> 7. **Net debt/EBITDA ratios exclude IFRS 16** and so are not computed on the headline EBITDA from FY20 `[sourced]` 4T20 p.2 fn 4; see [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-debt-fy14-fy20]].

## Key Takeaways

- ==Net sales rose from 187.1bn (FY14) to 331.1bn (FY20), a 10.0% CAGR `[calc]`, but FY14 organic growth was about nil and FY19 only +0.9%; M&A (Canada Bread, Donuts Iberia, Bimbo QSR, Mankattan) and FX explain most of it.==
- Gross margin held at 52.7%–54.0%; the improvement came from operating leverage and restructuring: operating margin 5.5% (FY14) to 7.7% (FY20); EBITDA margin 9.8% to 11.0% (FY18, like-for-like) then 13.0% / 13.7% on IFRS 16.
- ==FY19 Adj. EBITDA has two valid values (33,426 pre-IFRS 16; 37,873 IFRS 16). Always state the basis before computing growth or multiples; FY14–FY18 are pre-IFRS 16 and cannot be spliced with FY19+ headline figures.==
- Regional re-mapping in 2017 (China from Mexico to EAA) and the RA19 reclassification (+970 FY17, +1,054 FY18 sales) mean first-reported regional series do not tie to later comparatives; use the audited segment notes cited here.
- North America grew from 47% to 52% of the four regions' sales (49% in FY19) and became the margin driver after the 2018 VSP; Mexico stayed the highest-margin region; Latin America lost money at operating level in six of seven years.
- There are no regional volumes in any filing. The only volume series is group tonnes of packaged product (3.85m t FY14 to 5.03m t FY20), a production figure with a moving perimeter; sales per tonne is a price/mix ceiling proxy, not a price series.
