# Grupo Bimbo — IFRS Treatments, Adjusted EBITDA and Non-Recurring Items

How Grupo Bimbo defines its non-IFRS measures (Adjusted EBITDA, net debt, leverage ratios, free cash flow, ex-FX growth), what IFRS 16, the multi-employer pension plans (MEPPs) and discontinued operations do to the reported numbers, and every restatement found. Units: MXN millions unless stated. Source short names: **EEFF25** = audited FY25 statements and notes; **RA25** = FY25 BMV annual report (form page "n de 205"); **RA23** = FY23 BMV annual report (statements pages numbered within the statements; PDF p.256–361); "nQyy release" = earnings release (printed page).

## 1. Adjusted EBITDA (UAFIDA Ajustada)

- **Definition (annual report glossary)**: profit before financing, taxes, depreciation, amortisation, impairment, MEPP provision and other items that do not involve cash. Management uses it as an indicator of operating results and financial condition; it is not an IFRS measure and is not comparable with other companies' figures `[sourced]` RA25 p.12.
- **Definition (segment note)**: operating income plus depreciation, amortisation, impairment and other non-cash items, "mainly adjustments for valuation of multi-employer plans"; differs from the "Conformed EBITDA" used in loan covenants (note 13) `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.86 note (2).
- **Definition (releases)**: earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortisation, impairments and MEPPs `[sourced]` 4Q25 release p.1 footnote 2. In FY21 and in the 1Q22–3Q22 releases the wording had no "impairments" `[sourced]` 4Q21 release p.2 footnote 2; 1Q22 release p.1 footnote 2. The reconciliation below shows impairment was already added back in FY21.
- **Segment (regional) figures exclude inter-company royalties**; the consolidated total eliminates them `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.86 note (1); each release below the regional tables.

## 2. IFRS 16 (leases)

**Accounting policy.** Right-of-use assets and lease liabilities are recognised for leases other than short-term (under 12 months) and low-value (under US$5,000) leases, whose payments go straight to the income statement (so they stay inside EBITDA) `[sourced]` EEFF25 note 3(j), p.28–29. Leases are presented as capitalised operating and finance leases (note 9) `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.28.

- Reported Adjusted EBITDA is stated ==after the IFRS 16 benefit== (rent moves from operating costs to depreciation and interest). The company's leverage ratios "do not consider the IFRS 16 effect" `[sourced]` RA25 p.19 note (2). The ex-IFRS 16 EBITDA amount is not published; the ratios are matched, within rounding, by subtracting right-of-use depreciation from reported EBITDA in each of FY23, FY24 and FY25 for both net and total debt `[calc]` (see [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-leverage]]). This is an inferred method, not a disclosed definition `[unverified]`.
- Net debt as defined by the company excludes lease liabilities (34,790 at Dec-25). Including them against reported EBITDA gives 3.03x at Dec-25 and 2.80x at Jun-26 `[calc]`.
- Right-of-use depreciation is 25% of FY25 total D&A (6,142 / 24,838) `[calc]`; ROU assets and lease liabilities are in US-dollar and euro as well as peso, so lease liabilities move with FX (conversion effects of (3,051) on operating leases in 2025) `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.43.
- In the 2026 free-cash-flow definition, Adjusted EBITDA "without IFRS 16" is the starting point `[sourced]` 2Q26 release p.2 footnote 3; see section 6.

## 3. Multi-employer pension plans (MEPPs)

**What they are.** Bimbo Bakeries USA participates in union multi-employer plans administered by joint trusts. If other employers leave without paying their withdrawal liability, the unfunded amount is spread over the remaining employers `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.78–79.

**Accounting.**
- Plans are treated as defined-contribution (contributions expensed) unless there is enough information to treat them as defined benefit or the group's exit is "highly probable", in which case a provision at present value of estimated future cash outflows (discounted at the risk-free rate) is recognised under IAS 37 in other long-term liabilities `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.79 (note 18) and p.34 (accounting policy).
- Annual contributions expensed in general expenses: 2,430 (2023), 2,609 (2024), 2,700 (2025); 2,406 expected for 2026 (2,419.7 per the 1Q26 filing) `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.79; BMV Mar-26 pension note.
- Exit liabilities on the balance sheet (other long-term liabilities): 2,033 (Dec-23), 1,205 (Dec-24), 985 (Dec-25) `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.79 note 19.
- The provision is revalued each year; changes sit in operating income and are **added back/removed in Adjusted EBITDA** via the "other non-cash items" line above.

## 6. Other definitions used in the releases

- **Net debt** = total financial debt (short-term plus long-term, excluding lease liabilities) minus cash; ties to the disclosed net debt: Dec-25 145,128 = 153,663 − 8,535; Mar-26 137,751; Jun-26 137,097 `[calc]` / `[sourced]` 2Q26 release p.6; see [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-leverage]].
- **Net debt / Adjusted EBITDA** uses EBITDA that "does not consider the IFRS 16 effect" `[sourced]` RA25 p.19; 4Q25 release p.1 footnote 3. The 2Q22, 3Q22, 1Q23 and 2Q23 releases also state that the ratio excludes Ricolino `[sourced]` 2Q22 release p.6; 3Q22 release p.6; 1Q23 release p.5; 2Q23 release p.5.
- **Free cash flow** (2026 definition): Adjusted EBITDA without IFRS 16, minus capex (acquisitions excluded), taxes and net interest paid, plus the change in working capital of the period `[sourced]` 2Q26 release p.2 footnote 3. FY21 definition: free cash flow before acquisitions, dividends and share buybacks `[sourced]` 4Q21 release p.2 footnote 5. See [[bimbo-cash-flow-and-fcf]].
- **Ex-FX growth**: excludes the effect of translating the results of subsidiaries outside Mexico; the 2022–2Q23 releases quote North America in US dollars instead `[sourced]` 4Q25 release p.2 footnote 4; 4Q21 release p.3.
- **Return on equity / capital**: adjusted for MEPP (and for Ricolino in FY22) `[sourced]` 4Q22 release p.2 footnote 3; RA25 p.19 note (1).
- **Share count**: 4,304,744,119 shares at Dec-25 after 22,738,214 treasury shares `[sourced]` EEFF25 note 20; weighted-average 4,310,531 thousand for FY25 `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.13.
- **Hyperinflation (IAS 29)**: Argentina and Venezuela are hyperinflationary; monetary-position gain/loss in the income statement: (25) FY21, (34) FY22, (73) FY23, (103) FY24, 50 FY25 `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.23, p.26 (accounting policies), p.13; RA23 EEFF p.8. The Venezuelan investments are classified as equity-instrument financial assets at fair value `[sourced]` RA25 p.85.
- **New standards**: IFRS 18 (presentation and disclosure in financial statements) applies from 1-Jan-2027 with new income-statement categories and required subtotals; amendments to IFRS 9/7 and to electricity-contract accounting apply from 1-Jan-2026; no material impact stated `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.21; BMV Jun-26 policy notes. IFRS 18 will require management-defined performance measures such as Adjusted EBITDA to be reconciled in the notes (general feature of the standard; not discussed in the company's text `[unverified]`).

## Key Takeaways

- ==Adjusted EBITDA = operating income + D&A + impairment + MEPP/other non-cash items; it does not add back restructuring== (6.3bn in FY24, 3.9bn in FY25), so it is a conservative EBITDA `[sourced]`.
- Reported EBITDA benefits from IFRS 16; the company's 2.7x leverage (Dec-25) equals net debt over EBITDA less ROU depreciation (inferred), while net debt plus leases over reported EBITDA is 3.0x `[calc]`.
- ==FY2022 profit is distorted by two one-offs== (MEPP reversal ~US$0.9bn and the Ricolino disposal); FY2021 was restated for Ricolino (net sales −10.1bn) `[sourced]`.
- Restructuring has been a recurring cost every year (1.7–6.3bn), peaking in FY24 with the closure of 11 bakeries; a normalised operating income should treat part of it as ongoing `[calc]`.
- 1Q26 contains an undisclosed gain on an associate sale in EBITDA and net income; do not annualise 1Q26 margin (14.0%) or growth (+15.2% ex-FX EBITDA).
