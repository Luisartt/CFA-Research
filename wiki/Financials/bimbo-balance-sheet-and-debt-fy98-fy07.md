# Grupo Bimbo — Balance Sheet, Debt, Capex and Payouts FY1998–FY2007

Year-end balance sheet, interest-bearing debt, leverage, capital structure events, capex and dividends for the ten years before the IFRS series in [[bimbo-balance-sheet-and-leverage]]. Units: ==MXN millions== in the constant pesos of each report's own December (Mexican GAAP / NIF, inflation-restated), unless stated. Companion articles: [[bimbo-income-statement-fy98-fy07]] and [[bimbo-history-1998-2007]]. Related: [[bimbo-capital-allocation]], [[bimbo-capital-structure-and-debt]], [[bimbo-shareholder-returns]], [[ciq-debt-evolution-2019-2026]].

**Citation key.** `p.` = PDF page index. AR98 … AR07 = `filings/annual/INFORME ANUAL 1998.pdf` … `INFORME ANUAL 2007.pdf` (AR01 = `..._2001_0.pdf`; `_1` is identical; AR07 is identical to `filings/bmv/GrupoBimbo_BVM_RA_20080923_esp_0.pdf`). BMV02 = `filings/bmv/GrupoBimbo_BVM_RA_20020624_esp_0.pdf` (FY2001); BMV03 = the 26-Jun-2003 filing (FY2002).

> [!warning] Comparability
> - Every column is in its own year's pesos and is re-stated in the next report; do not subtract adjacent columns (see the restatement ladder in [[bimbo-income-statement-fy98-fy07]]). Ratios within a column are safe.
> - The statement of cash flows is the Mexican "statement of changes in financial position" through FY2007 (NIF B-2 cash-flow statement only from 1-Jan-2008) [sourced] AR07 p.133. Items below are the company's lines, not a US-style cash-flow statement.
> - **FY2000:** Bulletin D-4 adds a deferred-tax liability (MXN 1,455m) and a charge of MXN 1,747m to equity; FY1998–FY1999 have no deferred tax [sourced] AR00 p.24, p.27.
> - **FY2002–FY2003:** the Weston acquisition adds "brands and rights of use" (MXN 4,512m) and goodwill; the FY2003 report re-splits the FY2002 balance (goodwill 5,448 vs 3,260 originally; brands 3,164 vs 4,512) so the 2002 split between the two lines is unreliable [sourced] AR02 p.32; AR03 p.40.
> - **FY2005:** goodwill stops amortising (B-7); the 2005 report moves some "other assets" into goodwill (MXN 310m reclass shown in AR06) [sourced] AR05 p.34; AR06 p.42.
> - **FY2006–FY2007:** current portion of debt jumps (3,112 in 2006; 2,605 in 2007) because the 2007 and 2008 certificates fall due; do not read it as new borrowing.

### 2.4 Currency of the debt

- Dollar debt (mostly Mexico-booked): USD 464m (1999), 425m (2000), 540m (2001), ~USD 1,201m at 31-Mar-2002 after the bridge loan [sourced] BMV02 p.17. Net foreign-currency liability of the Mexican companies: USD 426m (1998), 202m (1999), 335m (2001, ex-US), 371m (2002), 233m (2003), 149m (2004), 52m (2007) [sourced] AR98 p.20; AR99 p.31; AR02 p.47; AR04 p.45; AR07 p.126.
- Mexican-peso funding from 2002: certificates MXN 6,500m, i.e. ~78% of 2005 debt (6,500 / 8,339) [calc] AR05 p.48. The company used the local market explicitly "to reduce exchange-rate risk" [sourced] AR02 p.6.
- Mrs. Baird's and later Weston debt was designated a hedge of the net investment in the US, so FX on that debt bypasses P&L and goes to equity (MXN 447.7m credit in 2002; (60.9m) in 2001; 269.0m charge in 1998) [sourced] AR02 p.38; AR98 p.16.

## Key Takeaways

- Bimbo ran at or near net cash in FY1999–FY2000 (net debt −0.07x EBITDA at end-2000), then twice levered up on discretionary decisions: a MXN 4.1bn tender offer (2001) and the USD 610m Weston purchase (2002), peaking at 2.1x net debt/EBITDA.
- ==The Weston bridge (USD 680m) was refinanced in five months== with MXN 6,500m of mxAAA/AAA(mex) peso certificates; by FY2004 leverage was 0.8x and by FY2007 0.25x, with debt at MXN 6.0bn against equity of 28.5bn.
- Capex was cut to 1.5–2.1% of sales in FY2002–FY2003 and rose to 5–6% by FY2005–FY2007; capex/D&A reached 1.9x in FY2007, before the larger investment cycle of the 2010s.
- Dividends were modest (payout 12–26% of EPS in normal years); the only large distributions were exceptional (Dec-1998, Nov-2004) and the 2001 tender offer dwarfed ten years of ordinary dividends.
- Equity moved only through three events: a 4-for-1 split (1998), two primary issues (1998, 1999) and a 17% buyback with cancellation (2001–2002); 1,175.8m shares have been outstanding ever since.
- Balance sheets are in own-year pesos and the FY2002 goodwill/brand split and FY2007 cash differ across reports; use ratios within a column and the company's reported net debt/EBITDA, not cross-year differences.
