## Key Takeaways

- CIQ year-end balance sheets are complete for 2019-2025 (including Dec-24) and tie to the 4T25 release totals within rounding.
- CIQ Total Debt / Net Debt include IFRS 16 leases (MXN 34.8 bn at Dec-25); strip them before comparing with the company's 2.7x Net Debt / Adj. EBITDA.
- After stripping leases, CIQ net debt still exceeds company net debt by MXN 66 mn to 2.8 bn (largest at Dec-23 and Dec-25), all in CIQ long-term debt.
- Negative working capital (current ratio 0.71x at Dec-25) and negative tangible book value (MXN −16.0 bn) are structural features of the balance sheet.
- The pension/post-retirement line collapsed in 2022 (33.1 → 11.5 bn), coinciding with the MEPP provision reversal.
- Jun-26: cash 16.6 bn, CIQ net debt 173.1 bn, ex-lease 139.8 bn [sourced]/[calc].
