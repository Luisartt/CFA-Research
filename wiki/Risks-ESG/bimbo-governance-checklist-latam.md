## Key Takeaways

- Governance is a **controlled-company profile**: 72.37% strategic holders (LSEG) / 72.03% (RA25), 27.63% free float, one share one vote, 38.9% independent board, executive family chair with casting vote. Overall rubric score 31/64 (48%) [assumption].
- The strongest section is committees (Audit fully independent, seven meetings, chair elected by AGM); the weakest is **takeover and minority protection (1/10)**: board veto over any 3%+ transfer, 30% tender-offer rule, 10% minority thresholds that all identified institutions combined (7.40%) cannot reach [calc].
- Related-party flow is modest (2.3% of sales, MXN 9.8bn FY2025) but includes non-associate related parties (Frexport MXN 1.7bn) whose ownership is undisclosed, and the conflict-of-interest policy does not cover directors or shareholders.
- Company-level inconsistency: a 3Q22 investor deck states a control group of 81% and a 19% float; every register and LSEG source since shows 72-73% and 27-28% [sourced].
- LSEG's governance grade (A-) is a policy-presence measure; S&P CSA governance is 46/100 — cite both when arguing governance in the report.
- Pay governance gaps (no say on pay, no claw-back, no TSR link, pay ratio omitted) and LSEG's UNGC-violation flags (FY2022, FY2024) are the evidence-backed ESG red flags.
