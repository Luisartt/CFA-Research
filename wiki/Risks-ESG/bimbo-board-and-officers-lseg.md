## Key Takeaways

- ==7 of 18 directors (38.9%) are independent==; all 11 others carry a family surname or private-business tie, and eight of them joined between 2022 and 2025 at a mean age of 47.5 [calc].
- The Executive Chairman (Servitje Montull, ex-CEO) has no lead independent director counterweight (LSEG: no lead independent director, chairperson not independent) and the board chair has the casting vote [sourced] ESG-G; bylaws Art. 21 and 24 (see [[bimbo-governance-checklist-latam]]).
- Audit is fully independent (5/5) and met seven times in 2025; Nominations is 50% independent and chaired by a family director; Finance (delegated M&A authority) is 1/7 independent [calc].
- 2025 board attendance was 99.0% with one absence (the Audit chair) [calc]; FY2024 attendance and all individual pay are undisclosed in LSEG.
- LSEG corroborates the 2025 exits of CEO Pamias, Deputy CEO Bendix and BBU head Gavin but gives no reasons; the CEO start date in LSEG (2026-06-10) conflicts with November 2025 and should not be used.
- Pay governance gaps (no say on pay, no claw-back, no TSR link, pay ratio omitted) are structural, not new [sourced] ESG-G; GRI 2-21.
