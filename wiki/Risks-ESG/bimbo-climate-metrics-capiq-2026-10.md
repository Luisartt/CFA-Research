## Key Takeaways

- The climate exposure that reaches the P&L is wheat: 60 bp of margin in 2022; sugar is small and no quantified sensitivity exists for weather events.
- Scope 2 is almost solved (about 97% renewable, Scope 2 −73% since 2021); Scope 3 is 8-10m tCO2e and the target is the hard one.
- Emissions series have two bases; compare only within a basis.
- The sustainability-linked bonds cost about Ps. 6m in step-up; the contradiction between reported Scope 3 progress and the missed target is open.
- 101 plants are in high water stress; new 2025 risks are water concessions, GHG regulation and insurance cost; no climate capex or carbon cost is disclosed.
- Gross margin moves against wheat with about a one-year lag (r = −0.74 on 2017-2025, n = 9); 2022 cost 197 bp of gross margin when average wheat rose 26.5%; 2026 wheat is running +17%, so pressure into 2027 is the risk.
- Hedge book at end-2025: about Ps. 315m, 64% wheat; about 100% of 2H25 needs and more than half of 1Q26 were hedged.
- Two runs of the same prompt disagree (101 vs 89 water-stressed sites, regional Scope 3 totals): use group totals and the filings.
- Mexico's cap-and-trade system is the one climate rule management says will hit profits; its operating rules are still unpublished.
