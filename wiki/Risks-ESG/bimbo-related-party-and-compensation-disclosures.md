## 5. Historical precedent: Canada Bread (pre-acquisition, 90% owned by Maple Leaf)

- Canada Bread paid Maple Leaf Foods management fees under an Intercompany Management Services Agreement (from 1-Jan-2012) of **CAD 51.0m (2013), 49.3m (2012), 47.4m (2011)**, cost-based [sourced] `CanadaBread_2014-03-31_AR_Periodo-2013-12-31` p.10.

## Key Takeaways

- Related-party purchases and services reached MXN 9.3bn in FY2025 (2.2% of net sales; 4.6% of cost of sales) after a 10.7bn peak in 2024; the two biggest exposures are Beta San Miguel (sugar, 8%-owned) and Frexport (not an associate) [sourced/calc].
- ==Frexport's purchases nearly doubled in 2024 (+80%) with no stated reason and no disclosed owner==, a diligence item for the governance section of the report [sourced/calc].
- Net payables to related parties are modest (MXN 1.15bn) and shrinking; all deals are described as at market terms and approved through the Audit Committee [sourced].
- Reported key-management pay is 922 (2025) but the definition changed after 2021 (restated 2021 from 845 to 658), so the 2015-2019 series (1,254-1,789 including post-retirement benefits) is not comparable with the 2022-2025 series (596-922) [sourced].
- Board fees per session rose 48% from 2018 to 2026 (abroad rate +124%); the VEAB virtual-share plan pays in cash 30 months after grant, linking executive pay to the BMV price without dilution [sourced/calc].
- Open items: owners of Frexport, FY2021-FY2022 fee totals, and the bridge between the two 2018 pay definitions (1,789 vs 767).
