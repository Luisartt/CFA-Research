## Central thesis (team, 2026-11-02)

**With acquisitions and the peso stripped out of growth, and costs that Bimbo has already shown it can absorb, Bimbo's value hinges on one thing: whether new management can expand margins on its own in 2027. Until we see it, the price already pays for what we know.**

How the pillars hold it up: pillar 1 is "acquisitions and the peso stripped out of growth" (about 3% organic growth, 1.46 of 4.75 points of FY2027E growth is currency); pillar 2 is "costs that Bimbo has already shown it can absorb" (raw materials +279 bp of sales in FY2021-FY2023 against +76 bp in FY2027E; gross margin -58 bp in FY2027E; wheat 35% higher for good costs about MXN 11.5 of the target); pillar 3 is "whether new management can expand margins on its own in 2027" (114 of the 120 bp come from two lines that fall almost to zero). Pillar 3 is the hinge; pillars 1 and 2 are what the team already knows. Status: the team put the title and two opening paragraphs in report v12; the meaning of "on its own" (margin gain without those two lines) are open `[team]`; the "price already pays" claim holds on the return, not on the level: at MXN 64.85 the 12-month return to the target plus the MXN 1.12 dividend is 3.7% against a cost of equity of 12.26% `[calc]` Valuacion_12m.

## One-line thesis

We rate Grupo Bimbo a **HOLD** because reported growth is flattered by acquisitions and currency, input costs are covered into the first half of 2027 and absorbable to 2030, and the new management inherits a recovery whose next step, in 2027, is still unproven; the 12-month target of MXN 66.13 returns 14.8% at the reference price of MXN 58.60 and 3.7% at the 27-Oct close of MXN 64.85, including the MXN 1.12 dividend, against a cost of equity of 12.26% `[calc]` TP12m_v2 Valuacion_12m. ## Pillars

### Pillar 1 (against buying) — Reported growth is flattered by acquisitions and FX

- **Claim:** the growth that Bimbo reports in pesos is not the growth the business earns by itself: part is bought and part is translated. The growth the model can defend ex-currency and ex-acquisitions is 3.3% in FY2027E, against 4.7% in 9M26 and 4.75% reported in pesos in FY2027E `[calc]` TP12m Organic_pass, Regional_FX.
- **Evidence:**
  - Bought: 24 deals from 2021 to mid-2026; MXN 40,987m paid in FY2021-FY2025, 69% of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA `[sourced]` RA25 pp.220-223; `wiki/Company/bimbo-acquisitions.md`, `research/thesis-a-working-draft.md` (count from the wiki); acquisitions explain about 1.15 pp of the 4.68% ex-FX growth of 9M26, a quarter of it (team method, sheet Organic_pass) `[calc]`.
  - Reported against organic in 9M26: Latin America 14.8% vs 7.5%, Europe-Asia-Africa 7.4% vs 4.4%; North America 1.0% still includes Stone House `[sourced]` 1Q26-3Q26 releases `[calc]`.
  - Management: growth "from price/mix, volume and, to a lesser extent, acquisitions"; Wickbold leaves the inorganic base from 4Q26 `[guidance]` 3Q26 call (team transcript from Zoom subtitles, to verify).
  - Translated: of the 4.75% peso growth of FY2027E, 3.29 pp is ex-FX and 1.46 pp is currency; the currency contribution is 1.20 / 1.05 / 0.51 pp in FY2028E-FY2030E and about -4 pp in FY2026E (sales -0.07% in pesos) `[calc]` TP12m Regional_FX. North America grows 3.8% in pesos and 0.9% in dollars in FY2027E `[calc]`.
  - The model's USD/MXN is 18.21 in 2027 against 18.48 spot (16-Oct-2026); with the Banxico survey average of 17.77 FY2027E sales fall 1.5%, EBIT 2.5% and EPS 3.2% `[assumption]` TP12m Assumptions; `[calc]` run of 2026-10-25.
  - Capital behind the growth: net debt including leases / EBITDA 1.89x (FY2022), 3.22x (FY2024), 3.03x (FY2025); tangible ROIC 17.7% to 11.9% (FY2022-FY2025) against the 15.78% the terminal value assumes `[calc]` TP12m.
- **Consensus view:** mean revenue MXN 426.3bn (FY2026E) and 445.3bn (FY2027E), +4.5% in pesos, in line with the model's +4.75% `[sourced]` CIQ consensus 20-Sep-2026 (`data/capitaliq/consensus.csv`) `[calc]`. The gap is in the composition, not the level.
- **Why the market is wrong (team, 2026-10-28: against both the price and the analysts):** against the price, the share rallied to MXN 64.85 after the 3Q26 results, on growth that is partly bought and partly translated; with the TP12m_v2 target of MXN 66.13 it now trades 1.9% below the target, so this argument against the price needs the team's review; against the analysts, a mean target of MXN 70.17 (+20% on 58.60) implies about 7.4x FY2027E EBITDA at the same claims, against 7.0x for our target and 6.6x in our terminal value, a re-rating that 3% organic growth does not support `[calc]` approximate: claims of MXN 196,098m; method of the analysts' targets unknown `[unverified]`.
- **Model link:** `seg_*_growth`, USD/MXN path and `Organic_pass` in the TP12m workbook; the model already projects organic growth only after 2026.
- **Value:** the target does not move with the acquisition share (the model is organic-only), but it does with currency and returns: DCF MXN 56.37 at 1.0% long-run depreciation, 60.18 at 1.7% (base), 63.98 at 2.3%; with the terminal return on new capital at the FY2025 level of 11.9% the target is MXN 54.47 instead of 66.13 `[calc]`.
- **Against:** the peso translation is real cash for a peso investor; the model already carries the organic number, so the pillar flags the quality of growth and not a lower target; the CEO says acquisitions matter only "to a lesser extent".
- **Kill criteria (proposed, team to confirm):** growth ex-FX and ex-acquisitions above 4% for two quarters in a row; or no large acquisition for the next four quarters together with leverage below 3.0x.

### Pillar 2 (against selling) — Costs can rise without breaking the margin: Bimbo has already absorbed a shock almost four times larger (v3.4, advisor-approved 2026-11-03; the five bullets are in report/sections/01-investment-summary.md; the "hedged into 1H27" and "absorbable to 2030" claims are dropped)

- **Claim:** the wheat shock arrives in FY2027E, covered until mid-year and with a bounded effect afterwards; the gross margin of 52.1%-52.2% projected to FY2030E stays inside the FY2021-FY2025 range of 51.4%-53.5% `[calc]` TP12m IS rows 6-8.
- **Evidence:**
  - Raw materials are 30.4% of sales and 63.8% of cost of sales (FY2025); wheat, priced in dollars, is the main input `[sourced]` EEFF25 p.82.
  - CFO: main raw materials fully hedged for 4Q26 and almost 100% for 1H27; Bimbo never hedges 15 months ahead; 2027 is "a challenge" for cost of sales `[guidance]` 3Q26 call (team transcript, to verify against an official source).
  - Lag: raw materials / sales follow the prior-year wheat price (elasticity 0.109, FY2018-FY2025, R-squared 0.70 against 0.48 same-year, 8 annual points); wheat -14.0% in 2025 lowers the ratio from 30.4% to 29.9% in FY2026E, the +25.9% of 2026 lands in FY2027E (30.6%; gross margin -58 bp to 52.1%) `[calc]` TP12m Assumptions rows 272-293; wheat `[sourced]` IMF/FRED PWHEAMTUSDM.
  - Size: one year of -100 bp costs MXN 0.65 per share (1.0% of the target); wheat at the Sep-2026 price of US$247 adds 0.42 pp of sales from FY2028E and takes MXN 4.74 off the target (FY2030E gross margin 51.8%); wheat 35% higher for good adds 1.02 pp, about MXN 11.5 (FY2030E gross margin 51.2%) `[calc]`.
  - History: gross margin lost 206 bp FY2021-FY2023, recovered 125 bp in FY2024 and gave back 30 bp in FY2025; raw materials were 32.8% of sales in FY2023 against 30.6% projected `[calc]` TP12m IS; EEFF25 p.82.
- **Consensus view:** gross margin 52.0% for FY2027 (CIQ, 20-Sep-2026), in line with the model's 52.1% `[sourced]`; no structural step down.
- **Why the market is wrong (team, 2026-10-28: there is no market gap on the numbers; the pillar is the argument against selling):** consensus gross margin is 52.0% for FY2027 and 52.05% for FY2028 against 52.1% in the model `[sourced]` CIQ consensus 20-Sep-2026 `[calc]`. The team agrees with the street and argues that the wheat shock is delayed, bounded and absorbable, so it does not justify a SELL. Considered and not used: that the market over-reacted to wheat in September (the share fell 7.3% against -1.9% for the IPC while FAO wheat export prices rose 6.3% in the month) because the cause cannot be shown and the share recovered 18% by 27-Oct `[calc]`.
- **Model link:** raw-material elasticity and wheat path in `Assumptions` rows 272-293 (wheat held at US$217.9/t from FY2028E).
- **Value:** the wheat-at-spot case is -4.74 and the 35% case about -11.5 on the MXN 66.13 target `[calc]` (linear scaling of -11.30 per 100 bp).
- **Against:** labour, depreciation and other lines absorbed about 60% of the raw-material relief of FY2023-FY2025 (1.44 of 2.39 pp on the unrounded EEFF25 amounts), and that recovery mixes price, mix and currency `[calc]` `wiki/Thesis/bimbo-evidence-vs-mentor-tests.md`; beyond 2027 "absorbable" rests on the flat-wheat method, not on management; the gross margin vs wheat link is r = -0.74 with n = 9 and the lag was chosen after the data `[calc]`; this thesis reverses the 19-Oct downside thesis on wheat.
- **Kill criteria (proposed, team to confirm):** 2H27 coverage disclosed as low or a 2027 guide that cannot absorb wheat; FY2027 gross margin below 51.5%, the FY2023 low.

### Pillar 3 (against buying) — The new management inherits a recovery, and 2027 is its first real test

- **Claim:** of the 120 bp that the model adds to the adjusted EBITDA margin between FY2025 and FY2027E (13.9% to 15.1%), 91 bp arrive in FY2026E, with nine months reported and the guide already raised; the remaining 29 bp in FY2027E are what the new management still has to prove, and they come from lines that the model assumes vanish `[calc]` TP12m IS; Segments.
- **Evidence:**
  - Who is new: Rodriguez Bas CEO since November 2025 (at Bimbo since October 2021, Barcel Global); Koehrsen head of Bimbo Bakeries USA since January 2026; Pamias, Bendix and Gavin left in 2025; the CFO has held the post since 2017 and Servitje is Executive Chairman; no strategy pivot since the appointment `[sourced]` RA25 pp.76-77, 178-180; `wiki/Thesis/bimbo-ceo-rodriguez-bas-and-guidance-capiq-2026-10.md`.
  - Inherited: North America margin 5.9% (4Q24) and 10.4% (3Q25), before November 2025; annual 12.2% (FY2021), 8.4% (FY2024), 9.0% (FY2025) `[sourced]` wiki; `[calc]` TP12m Segments.
  - Early delivery: group margin 15.7% in 3Q26 (+100 bp), fifth quarter above 14%; 3Q26 growth 3.6% ex-FX; net debt/EBITDA 2.4x; 2026 margin guide raised to +85 to +120 bp `[guidance]` 3Q26 call and release. Four quarters on a low base; FY2026E includes a one-off of about 11.5 bp (La Moderna) `[calc]`.
  - What the 29 bp need: 114 bp of the 120 bp come from other expenses (1.15% to 0.14% of sales) and integration costs (MXN 541m to zero); other expenses were 1.1%-1.5% of sales in FY2023-FY2025 `[calc]` TP12m IS; EEFF25 p.13. The CFO calls 2027 "a challenge" for cost of sales and the CEO expects more Wickbold pressure `[guidance]` 3Q26 call.
  - Promises: of 61 testable targets set on calls since 2011, 22 met, 9 mixed, 30 missed; the pledge to stay below 2x leverage held from December 2020 to 3Q23 `[calc]` `wiki/Thesis/bimbo-evidence-vs-mentor-tests.md`.
- **Consensus view:** adjusted EBITDA MXN 62.1 / 64.9 / 69.0bn for FY2026-FY2028 against the model's 63.3 / 67.6 / 70.4bn (+1.9% / +4.2% / +2.0%); FY2027 margin 14.6% against 15.1% in the model (+55 bp, about MXN 6.2 of the target) `[sourced]` CIQ consensus 20-Sep-2026, before the 3Q26 call `[calc]`.
- **Why the market is wrong (team, 2026-10-28: the market already pays in advance for a delivery that 2027 has not yet proven):** the share rose 10.7% between 16 and 27 October to MXN 64.85, now 1.9% below our 12-month target of MXN 66.13 (the 'already paid' reading rests on the return: 3.7% to the target plus dividend against 12.26%), while FY2027 consensus EPS was MXN 3.35 on 20-Sep, before the call; the P/E on it went from 17.5x to 19.4x `[calc]`. A public page of 28-Oct shows 3.33 with no upward revisions `[unverified]`. Counter-case: estimates may lag the call and rise, which would support the opposite reading; refresh the CIQ consensus.
- **Model link:** other expenses and integration costs in `IS`; EBITDA margin path (14.84% FY2026E, 15.12% from FY2027E) `[calc]`.
- **Value:** every 100 bp of margin kept for good is MXN 11.30 on the target (17%); 50 bp is MXN 5.65; other expenses held at 0.76% instead of 0.14% would take 62 bp and MXN 7.0 `[calc]`.
- **Against:** the 3Q26 margin of 15.7% is above the model's 15.1%; the CFO did not guide a margin decline for 2027; guidance has mostly been beaten since 2020; no figures isolate the CEO's contribution at Barcel Global `[sourced]`/`[guidance]`.
- **Kill criteria (proposed, team to confirm):** a 2027 guide implying a margin gain above 50 bp (toward BUY) or a flat margin (toward SELL); North America margin below 9% for two quarters; quantified long-term targets presented and met.
