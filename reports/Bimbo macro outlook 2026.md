# Peso relief meets Bimbo's 2027 cost squeeze

*Macro view as of 8-Oct-2026. [calc] = team calculation; WACC figures are **illustrative** (assumed beta 0.8, tax 30%, not team inputs).*

The macro case for a 12-month BUY rests on two things. First, the FX drag on peso-reported results is ending. Second, the multiple is depressed (**6.3x EV/EBITDA vs a 7.8x 10-year mean**, per team Capital IQ data). Macro does not supply demand growth. Mexican GDP grows about 1.5%, US bread volumes are shrinking, and the Fed is hiking with the US 10y above 5.2%. Input costs re-accelerate into 1H27. Our base case is a translation tailwind of +1 to +3pp in 2027 against roughly 150–240bp of gross-margin cost pressure before pricing. That makes BUY a valuation-and-execution call, not a macro-momentum call.

## Mexico grows slowly while wages outrun prices

GDP rose only **0.8% in 2025** ([La Jornada, 23-Feb-2026](https://www.jornada.com.mx/noticia/2026/02/23/economia/el-pib-de-mexico-crecio-08-por-ciento-en-2025)). After a 1Q26 contraction, 2Q26 grew **+1.4% q/q** with World Cup help ([MBN, 24-Aug-2026](https://mexicobusiness.news/trade-and-investment/news/mexicos-economy-grew-14-2q26)). Sources conflict on the size of the 1Q26 contraction (-0.6% vs -0.3%). Banxico forecasts **1.5% (2026) and 2.0% (2027)** ([Expansión, 26-Aug-2026](https://expansion.mx/economia/2026/08/26/banxico-economia-pib-2026-1-5)), while analysts expect 1.4% and 1.8% ([Forbes MX, Oct-2026](https://forbes.com.mx/sector-privado-baja-pronostico-de-inflacion-en-mexico-para-2026-y-sube-el-del-pib-sondeo-banxico/)). The World Bank's 2028 figure is **2.2%**, but it comes only from a secondary source ([MND, Oct-2026](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/news/imf-growth-forecast-mexico-2026/)).

September CPI was **3.45%**, with core at 3.75% and food at 4.57% ([México ¿cómo vamos?, 8-Oct-2026](https://mexicocomovamos.mx/publicaciones/2026/10/la-inflacion-anual-sube-a-3-45-en-septiembre-por-el-alza-de-la-no-subyacente/)). Banxico held at **6.50%** and expects inflation to reach target only in 4Q27 ([Banxico, 24-Sep-2026](https://www.banxico.org.mx/canales/%7B64E93EB1-E9FF-891E-60C6-9BBC812A50C6%7D.pdf)). The Citi survey's 2027 median is 6.50%, with a range of 6.0–7.25% ([El Financiero, 6-Oct-2026](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/mercados/2026/10/06/encuesta-citi-en-cuanto-cerrara-el-tipo-de-cambio-en-2026/)). Outlets disagree on whether September was the third or fourth straight hold.

Low-income demand gets support from a **13% minimum-wage increase** ([La Jornada, 2-Jan-2026](https://www.jornada.com.mx/noticia/2026/01/02/politica/entra-en-vigor-alza-de-13-al-salario-minimo-en-zona-fronteriza-libre-crece-5)) and record formal employment of **22.9m**, although that is only +1.6% y/y ([Infobae/EFE, 8-Oct-2026](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/10/08/el-empleo-formal-en-mexico-sube-en-septiembre-y-alcanza-record-de-229-millones-de-puestos/)). Other demand signals are soft. Consumer confidence fell to **45.1** ([UnoTV, 6-Oct-2026](https://www.unotv.com/negocios/confianza-consumidor-mexico-cae-inegi-economia-empleo-compras/)). ANTAD same-store sales rose 1.7% nominal in August ([ANTAD](https://antad.net/indicadores/crecimiento-nominal-en-ventas/)). Remittances fell **3.6% y/y in August**, and Banamex estimates they were down 13.8% in real pesos ([La Silla Rota, 1-Oct-2026](https://lasillarota.com/negocios/2026/10/1/remesas-caen-36-en-agosto-banamex-preve-recuperacion-moderada-531089.html)).

The **40-hour week** cuts maximum hours from 48 to 46 in 2027, falling to 40 by 2030 with no pay cut ([El Heraldo, 21-Sep-2026](https://heraldodemexico.com.mx/nacional/2026/9/21/la-jornada-laboral-baja-46-horas-el-de-enero-de-2027-no-podran-reducir-tu-sueldo-892260.html)). A consultancy estimates the cost per hour rises about 4% in 2027 and about 20% by 2030 ([WorkAxle](https://www.workaxle.com/es/blog/jornada-40-horas-mexico)). Sources disagree on the reform's effective date.

Trade risk is mostly indirect for Bimbo. The US **declined to renew USMCA** on 1-Jul-2026, so the agreement moves to annual reviews ([Thompson Hine, Jul-2026](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/07/ustr-issues-statement-on-status-of-usmca-review/)). A 10% Section 301 duty has applied to non-exempt Mexican goods since 24-Jul ([Federal Register, 28-Jul-2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/28/2026-15181/notice-of-actions-in-section-301-investigations-of-acts-policies-and-practices-of-various-economies)). Bimbo bakes locally, so the main channels are peso volatility and weak investment. FDI hit a record USD 35.0bn in 1H26, but **88.5% was reinvested earnings** ([SE, 24-Aug-2026](https://www.gob.mx/se/prensa/mexico-registra-un-maximo-historico-de-inversion-extranjera-directa-ied-para-un-primer-semestre-34-mil-968-millones-de-dolares-en-2026)), so nearshoring is stalling. The tax risk to watch through late October is the 8% snack IEPS; NGOs are pushing to raise it to 20% ([Expansión, 2-Sep-2026](https://expansion.mx/economia/2026/09/02/impuestos-sopas-maruchan-botanas-alcohol-ieps-2027)).

## The FX headwind fades and likely flips in 2027

Spot quotes on 8-Oct conflict, ranging from **18.01** ([ECB-derived](https://api.frankfurter.app/latest?from=USD&to=MXN,CAD,EUR,BRL)) to **18.19** at the close ([Milenio, 8-Oct-2026](https://www.milenio.com/negocios/cierre-del-dolar-cotizacion-peso-mexicano-hoy-dia-mes-2026)). The 2026 average to date is about 17.42, against 19.48 in the same period of 2025 [calc]. The Citi survey sees **18.00 at end-2026 and 18.50 at end-2027** ([El Financiero, 6-Oct-2026](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/mercados/2026/10/06/encuesta-citi-en-cuanto-cerrara-el-tipo-de-cambio-en-2026/)), which matches the covered-interest-parity forward of about 18.47 [calc]. Translation cut **-6.7pp** from 2Q26 sales growth (-2.2% reported vs +4.5% ex-FX, team data from the 2Q26 release). We estimate about -4.6pp in 3Q26, about -1.3pp in 4Q26 at spot, and **+1 to +3pp in 2027** on the Banxico-survey and Citi paths [calc]. The Banxico–Fed policy gap is a record-low ~250bp ([El Financiero, 6-Oct-2026](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/mercados/2026/10/06/encuesta-citi-en-cuanto-cerrara-el-tipo-de-cambio-en-2026/)), so the peso's carry support is thinning.

## US demand is the weakest link

On 16-Sep the Fed **hiked to 3.75–4.00%**, and its projections show 4.1% at end-2026 and end-2027 ([Fed SEP, 16-Sep-2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcprojtabl20260916.htm)). Conference Board confidence of **81.9** is the lowest since 2014 ([GV Wire/Reuters, 29-Sep-2026](https://gvwire.com/2026/09/29/us-consumer-confidence-dives-to-more-than-12-year-low-in-september/)). Center-store bread units fell **3.6%** ([Supermarket Perimeter/Circana](https://www.supermarketperimeter.com/articles/13986-better-for-you-becomes-bread-industrys-priority)). Flowers' branded volume fell **7.6%**, and it cited trade-down to private label ([Flowers 8-K, 20-Aug-2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001128928/000119312526359191/flo-ex99_1.htm)). The often-quoted "-9.5%" bread figure is a Flowers unit metric, not a category number.

Private label has reached a record **23.8% unit share** ([Bakery & Snacks, 9-Jul-2026](https://www.bakeryandsnacks.com/Article/2026/07/09/private-label-widens-lead-over-national-brands-in-2026-grocery-unit-sales/)). About **22% of households** include a GLP-1 user ([Grocery Dive/Numerator, 7-Aug-2026](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/glp1-weight-loss-drugs-medication-grocery-spend-numerator/827277/)). Oral GLP-1s priced at $149 a month ([Fierce Pharma, Jan-2026](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/novos-wegovy-pill-makes-us-debut-starter-dose-launching-149-month-cash-paying-patients)) should widen adoption. Food-at-home inflation is a modest **2.4% (2026) and 1.8% (2027)** ([USDA ERS, 25-Sep-2026](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings)), which limits pricing. On the positive side, wage growth has cooled to **3.0%** ([First Trust, 2-Oct-2026](https://www.ftportfolios.com/Commentary/EconomicResearch/2026/10/2/nonfarm-payrolls-increased-29,000-in-september)). Bimbo's +1.3% North America growth ex-FX in 2Q26 ([Baking Business, Jul-2026](https://www.bakingbusiness.com/articles/66636-bimbo-sales-profit-gains-accelerate-in-quarter)) beats its peers, but promotional intensity is the cost.

## Input costs peak in 1H27, not now

Dec-26 CBOT wheat is **$6.865**, about **49% below the March-2022 peak** but **+35% y/y**. The curve is in contango, with Dec-27 at $7.235 ([IndexBox/USDA AMS, 7-Oct-2026](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/usda-grain-futures-and-canola-bids-report-for-october-7-2026/); [Trading Economics, 8-Oct-2026](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/wheat)). KC HRW is at $7.385. Sugar is up **+24% y/y** ([Trading Economics, 8-Oct-2026](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/sugar)), and FAO's vegetable-oil index is up 18% ([FAO, 2-Oct-2026](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en)). US diesel averaged **$6.29/gal** in September, and EIA forecasts $4.49 for 2027 ([EIA STEO, Oct-2026](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/pdf/steo_full.pdf)). Mexico's voluntary diesel cap of about MXN 27/l shields the domestic fleet ([Medios Obson, 5-Oct-2026](https://mediosobson.com/2026/10/05/mas-de-8-mil-gasolineras-venden-el-diesel-en-menos-de-27-pesos-por-litro-profeco/)). Campbell's guides to **5–6% input inflation** for FY27 ([Investing.com, Sep-2026](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-campbells-q4-2026-revenue-miss-and-weak-outlook-hit-shares-93CH-4888096)). As hedges roll, we estimate a gross-margin headwind of about 150–240bp before pricing [calc]. Partial offsets come from eggs, natural gas and packaging resin.

## Rates cap the re-rating

The US 10y is **5.23%**, near its highest level since 2002 ([CNBC, 8-Oct-2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/us-treasury-yields-30-year-bond-auction.html)). The Mexico 10y is **9.32%** ([Milenio, 8-Oct-2026](https://www.milenio.com/negocios/cierre-del-dolar-cotizacion-peso-mexicano-hoy-dia-mes-2026)). Mexico's 5y CDS is about **98bp**, against a 52-week range of 78–114bp ([Investing.com, 1-Oct-2026](https://www.investing.com/rates-bonds/mexico-cds-5-years-usd-historical-data)). Mexico is one notch above junk after Moody's cut it to Baa3 ([Blackwood LCG](https://blackwoodlcg.com/Articulo-Calificacion-Moodys-Mexico-2026.html)). Damodaran puts Mexico's country risk premium at **2.72%** and the mature-market ERP at 4.20% ([Damodaran, Jul-2026](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/ctrypremJuly26.xlsx)).

On illustrative inputs, MXN WACC is about **9.8–10.6%** [calc]. A 50–100bp fall in Mexican yields would cut WACC by 0.4–0.9pp and lift EV by about 8–17%. That implies 6.8–7.4x EV/EBITDA, still below the 10-year mean [calc]. Bimbo's de-rating happened while CDS tightened, so its causes are mostly company-specific.

## LatAm mixed, Europe hawkish

Brazil is still easing, with the **Selic at 13.75%**. The BRL is up about 10% YTD, and the 25-Oct presidential runoff is a risk ([Rio Times, Sep-2026](https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazil-selic-cut-95-percent-copom-september-2026/)). Colombia hiked to **12.25%** as inflation hit 6.29% ([BanRep, 30-Sep-2026](https://www.banrep.gov.co/en/news/board-directors/september-2026)); COP quotes conflict. Peru's Lima CPI is 4.55% under El Niño pressure ([Infobae, 1-Oct-2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/01/la-inflacion-de-lima-metropolitana-alcanza-el-455-en-septiembre-y-se-acerca-a-su-mayor-nivel-en-15-anos/)). Argentina runs at **33.5% y/y** and stays under IAS 29 ([Infobae, 11-Sep-2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/09/11/despues-del-17-que-se-registro-el-mes-pasado-que-se-espera-para-la-inflacion-de-septiembre/)). In Europe, the ECB hiked to **2.50%** with euro-area inflation at 3.8% ([Eurostat, 2-Oct-2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-02102026-ap)), and the IMF projects euro-area growth of 0.9% in 2026 ([IMF, Jul-2026](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/weo/2026/update/july/english/text.pdf)).

## Tailwinds versus headwinds (12 months)

| Tailwinds | Headwinds / risks |
|---|---|
| FX translation turns positive in 2027 | Fed hikes; US 10y above 5.2% |
| Mexican real wages and minimum wage | US bread volumes, private label, GLP-1 |

| Valuation 1.5x below the 10y mean | 40-hour week; snack IEPS; USMCA reviews |
| Brazil easing | Sovereign downgrade; Andean and European tightening |

## Conclusion

A BUY has to look past the next two quarters. The cost peak in 1H27 coincides with the translation tailwind, so reported growth should improve before margins do. The most valuable macro signals to watch are the 3Q26 gross margin, Congress's IEPS vote (late October), the Fed's October and December decisions, and Bimbo's 2027 hedge disclosure.
